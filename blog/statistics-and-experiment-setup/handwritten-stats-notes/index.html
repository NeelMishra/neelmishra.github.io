    Handwritten Statistics & Experiment Setup Notes

    October 2026 · 6 min read · 9 Chapters (152 Handwritten Pages)

    These nine guides transcribe and expand 152 pages of handwritten OneNote statistics and experimental design notes into step-by-step explanations. Every chapter preserves the original examples, tables, figures, and worked numerical calculations while placing the original PDFs and page scans right alongside the text.

    Follow the path from exploratory data analysis to statistical inference

    The nine chapters divide naturally into three parts: Part I (Chapters 1–3) covers single-variable distributions, bivariate regression, sampling, and randomized experimental design; Part II (Chapters 4–5) builds probability theory, random variables, and the Central Limit Theorem; and Part III (Chapters 6–9) develops confidence intervals and hypothesis tests for means, matched pairs, variances, proportions, and two-way contingency tables.

    01
    Chapter 1: Examining Distributions

    EDA strategy, bar/Pareto charts, histograms, stemplots, time series, mean vs. median resistance, $1.5\times\text{IQR}$ outliers, sample variance $s^2$ ($n-1$ degrees of freedom), $68\text{–}95\text{–}99.7$ Normal rule, $z$-scores, and Normal quantile plots.

     02
    Chapter 2: Examining Relationships

    Explanatory vs. response variables, scatterplots, Pearson correlation $r$ and its 7 properties, least-squares regression ($\hat{y}=a+bx$), $r^2$, residual plots, influential points, lurking variables, causation criteria, and marginal vs. conditional distributions.

     03
    Chapter 3: Producing Data — Sampling & Experimental Design

    Observational studies vs. experiments, confounding, Simple Random Samples (SRS), stratified & multi-stage sampling (CPS), survey biases, factorial treatments, placebo & double-blinding, matched pairs, randomized block designs, and bias vs. variability.

     04
    Chapter 4: Probability & Sampling Distributions

    Discrete and continuous random variables, expected value, variance of correlated sums ($\sigma_{X+Y}^2 = \sigma_X^2 + \sigma_Y^2 + 2\rho\sigma_X\sigma_Y$ & portfolio diversification), Law of Large Numbers, $\mu_{\bar{x}}=\mu$, $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}$, and the Central Limit Theorem.

     05
    Chapter 5: Probability Theory — General Rules, Binomial & Poisson

    Disjoint vs. independent events, addition & multiplication rules, conditional probability, tree diagrams & Bayes' rule, the Binomial distribution $\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$ and Normal approximation, and the Poisson distribution $e^{-\mu}\mu^k/k!$.

     06
    Chapter 6: Introduction to Statistical Inference

    Confidence intervals $\bar{x} \pm z^*\sigma/\sqrt{n}$, sample size planning $n=(z^*\sigma/m)^2$, bootstrap intervals, significance tests ($H_0, H_a$, $P$-values), CI–test duality, multiple testing, statistical power curves, and Type I (FP) vs. Type II (FN) errors.

     07
    Chapter 7: Inference for Distributions

    Standard error $SE_{\bar{x}}=s/\sqrt{n}$, Student's $t(n-1)$ distribution, one-sample $t$ intervals & tests, matched pairs $t$ procedures, robustness guidelines, the sign test, Welch–Satterthwaite and pooled two-sample $t$ procedures, and the $F$ test for variances.

     08
    Chapter 8: Inference for Proportions

    Sampling distribution of $\hat{p}=X/n$, large-sample $z$ intervals vs. Plus Four (Wilson) intervals $\tilde{p}=(X+2)/(n+4)$, one-sample $z$ tests, worst-case sample size planning ($p^*=0.5$), comparing two proportions ($p_1-p_2$), pooled $z$ tests, and Relative Risk ($RR$).

     09
    Chapter 9: Inference for Two-Way Tables

    Contingency tables ($r \times c$), joint vs. conditional distributions, expected cell counts $(\text{row total}\times\text{col total})/n$, the Pearson Chi-Square statistic $X^2$ with $\text{df}=(r-1)(c-1)$, exact equivalence $X^2=z^2$ for $2\times 2$ tables, meta-analysis, and homogeneity vs. independence models.
