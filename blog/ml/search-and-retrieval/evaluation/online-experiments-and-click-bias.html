    Clicks Are Feedback, Not Relevance Labels

    September 2026 · 4 min read

    Before you start: Uncertainty and the experimental unit.

    The first result gets more clicks than the fifth. That might mean it is better. It might also mean more people saw it. A ranking system chooses exposure, then learns from the behavior that exposure creates. Without accounting for this loop, it can mistake its own previous decisions for evidence of user preference.

    A small model of position bias

    Let $E$ indicate examination and $C$ a click. In a simple position-based model, $P(C=1\mid q,d,i)=P(E=1\mid i)\,P(C=1\mid E=1,q,d)$. The first factor is exposure; the second is click attractiveness given exposure. Even the second is not identical to relevance: titles, images, and misleading promises can influence it.

    The items are equally attractive once seen, yet the first receives four times the click probability in this illustrative model.

    A non-click therefore needs context. Was the result visible? Did the user scroll? Did they stop after an earlier success? Did the answer appear directly on the page? Logging only query, item, and click loses the exposure information needed to interpret the event.

    What propensity weighting tries to correct

    If an item is observed with known positive propensity $e_i$, inverse propensity weighting upweights observed evidence by $1/e_i$. In the toy model, a click at examination probability 0.2 gets weight 5, while one at 0.8 gets weight 1.25. Under the corresponding assumptions, this corrects unequal exposure in expectation.

    $$\widehat V_{\mathrm{IPS}}=\frac1N\sum_{t=1}^{N}\frac{\pi(a_t\mid x_t)}{\mu(a_t\mid x_t)}\,r_t.$$

    This more general off-policy form compares a target policy π with the logging policy μ; it is not the same object as a position-examination weight. In a ranking application, define whether an action is an item, position assignment, or full slate, and record the propensity for that action. Multiplying arbitrary position estimates into a slate estimator does not establish unbiasedness.

    Support is essential: if the logging system never exposes an action the new policy would choose, the log cannot identify its reward by reweighting. Small propensities create high variance. Clipping weights can stabilize an estimate but introduces bias; report clipping and effective sample size. Estimated propensities add uncertainty and can be wrong when examination depends on more than position.

    Use randomized experiments to measure outcomes

    Randomly assign users or another suitable stable unit to control and treatment, and keep the assignment consistent when cross-session effects matter. Log eligibility, assignment, actual exposure, and outcomes separately. Check assignment ratios, missing events, and implementation parity before interpreting the result. Select the primary metric and duration logic in advance; ordinary fixed-horizon tests do not permit unlimited unadjusted peeking.

    Metric denominators should reflect randomized eligibility, not a post-treatment subset chosen because it looks favorable.

    Why offline and online results can disagree

    Offline relevance may improve while online engagement remains unchanged because users cannot notice the difference, latency increased, the test set mismatches traffic, or the changed slice is rare. An online gain can occur without a relevance gain because presentation improved. Investigate these mechanisms rather than declaring all offline evaluation useless or assuming the online experiment is broken.

    Interleaving can compare rankers by combining their results into one displayed list and attributing interactions under a specified protocol. It can be sensitive for preference comparisons, but it does not automatically estimate the business impact of deploying an entire new stack. Use the method that matches the decision, including its assumptions about attribution and user behavior.

    Check your understanding: A new ranker increases CTR but lowers successful purchases and raises returns. Did search improve?

    The answer depends on the declared objective, but CTR alone cannot justify the launch. The broader outcomes suggest clicks may have become less useful. Inspect intent and presentation changes alongside the guardrails.
