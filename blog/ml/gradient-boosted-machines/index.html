    Gradient Boosting — StatQuest Notes

    September 2026 · Four lectures · StatQuest companion notes

    Four companion notes for Josh Starmer’s StatQuest series on gradient boosting. Read them in the video order: first see the algorithm work, then derive its formulas.

    Each lecture keeps its worked example, with labeled trees, plots, and links to the relevant video moments.

    The four lectures

    01
    Regression: Main Ideas

    Why predicting residuals improves regression, shared leaf corrections, learning rate, and the sum of trees.

    02
    Regression: The Derivation

    Derive mean initialization, negative gradients, terminal regions, mean leaf corrections, and regression updates.

    03
    Classification: Main Ideas

    Log-odds, sigmoid probabilities, classification pseudo-residuals, leaf corrections, and sequential tree updates.

    04
    Classification: The Derivation

    Derive binary log loss, y minus p, initial log-odds, and Newton leaf values from a quadratic approximation.

    Follow the examples in order

    Lectures 1 and 2 predict weight. Lectures 3 and 4 predict whether someone likes a movie. The derivation lectures use smaller data sets to make every leaf calculation visible.

    The figures are redrawn from the teaching examples. Calculations retain precision where the videos round; the notes say when that changes a displayed number.
