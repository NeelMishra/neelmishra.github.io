    A study loop for each chapter

    1. Read the motivating example. Name the input, the desired output and the question the method is meant to answer.
    2. Work through the figure. Predict the next step before inspecting the result. Check at least one calculation by hand.
    3. Return to the source. Read the linked textbook material for the wider treatment, notation and exercises.
    4. Test your explanation. Change one assumption in the example and describe what should change in the result.

    These are independently written study notes with original examples and figures. Chapter pages identify their source material so you can move between the explanation and the authors’ manuscript. The series guide makes the available coverage explicit as the companion grows.
