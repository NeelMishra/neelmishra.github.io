    Anomaly Detection: Reading Guide

    September 2026 · 3 min read

    Follow random isolation paths, model sparse telemetry, and turn unusualness into a useful investigation queue.

    Reading order

    01
    Isolation Forest: Follow One Point Through a Forest

    Derive isolation path length and anomaly scores, understand subsampling, and distinguish raw scores from thresholded labels.

    02
    Model Sparse, Heavy-Tailed Telemetry Without Flagging Every Large Customer

    Choose the observation unit, normalize activity exposure, distinguish zeros from missing data, and validate anomaly models across tenant and time slices.

    03
    Turn Anomaly Scores into an Investigation Queue

    Choose score thresholds or review budgets, measure delayed outcomes, explain unusual features, and keep anomaly evidence separate from a maliciousness conclusion.

    How to use these notes

    Start with the worked example in each chapter. Calculate the answer before changing the interactive figure, then use the self-check to test the distinction the chapter teaches. The numerical examples are deliberately small enough to audit by hand; they are teaching examples, not production benchmarks.

    Follow the prerequisites when a concept is unfamiliar. Related notes link to existing foundations so each chapter can concentrate on its own question.
