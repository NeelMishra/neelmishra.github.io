    Vanishing and Exploding Gradient Problems Across Activation Functions

    October 2026 · 2 min read

    1. Forward pass

    $$a_j^{(l)} = \phi\!\bigl(z_j^{(l)}\bigr), \qquad z_i^{(l+1)} = \sum_{j=1}^{n_{\rm in}} W_{i,j}^{(l+1)} a_j^{(l)}.$$

    2. Backward recurrence with $\phi'\!\bigl(z^{(l)}\bigr)$

    With $\delta_j^{(l)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_j^{(l)}}$ and $g_j^{(l)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_j^{(l)}}$:

    $$g_j^{(l)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_j^{(l)}} = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\rm out}} \underbrace{\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(l+1)}}}_{\delta_i^{(l+1)}} \underbrace{\frac{\partial z_i^{(l+1)}}{\partial a_j^{(l)}}}_{W_{i,j}^{(l+1)}} = \sum_{i=1}^{n_{\rm out}} W_{i,j}^{(l+1)} \delta_i^{(l+1)}.$$
    $$\delta_j^{(l)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_j^{(l)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_j^{(l)}} \cdot \frac{\partial a_j^{(l)}}{\partial z_j^{(l)}} = g_j^{(l)} \cdot \phi'\!\bigl(z_j^{(l)}\bigr) = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n_{\rm out}} W_{i,j}^{(l+1)} \delta_i^{(l+1)}\right) \phi'\!\bigl(z_j^{(l)}\bigr).$$

    In 1D ($n_{\rm in} = n_{\rm out} = 1$, $h^{(l)} = a^{(l)}$):

    $$\delta^{(l)} = w^{(l+1)}\, \phi'\!\bigl(z^{(l)}\bigr)\, \delta^{(l+1)}, \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(l)}} = w^{(l+1)}\, \phi'\!\bigl(z^{(l+1)}\bigr)\, \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(l+1)}}.$$

    3. Unrolling across $L$ layers and maximum bound $M$

    $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(0)}} = \left(\prod_{l=1}^{L} w^{(l)}\, \phi'\!\bigl(z^{(l)}\bigr)\right) \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(L)}}.$$

    For $|w^{(l)}| = 1$ and $\bigl|\phi'(z)\bigr| \le M$:

    $$\left|\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(0)}}\right| = \left(\prod_{l=1}^{L} \bigl|\phi'\!\bigl(z^{(l)}\bigr)\bigr|\right) \left|\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(L)}}\right| \le M^L \left|\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h^{(L)}}\right|.$$
