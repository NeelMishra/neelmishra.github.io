    Weight Initialization in Deep Learning: Reading Guide

    September 2026 · 3 min read + interactive lab

    A connected guide with derivations, worked examples, original figures, and interactive experiments.

    Initialization sets the starting point of optimization. It must create different hidden features, keep forward signals and backward derivatives usable, and respect the architecture’s activation functions and residual paths. These chapters build the recipes from those requirements.

    How to study the experiments

    Read the worked calculation, predict what a control will change, then run the experiment. Values come from the equations or a specified small dataset; they are not fabricated benchmark curves. Every interactive result has text readouts, and the main explanation also works with the static figures.

    Keep these distinctions visible

    $n_{\rm in}$ and $n_{\rm out}$ count inputs and outputs of a layer. Weight variance, activation variance, and activation second moment are different quantities. Most scaling derivations describe an expectation at initialization under independence assumptions, not an exact guarantee for every sampled network.
