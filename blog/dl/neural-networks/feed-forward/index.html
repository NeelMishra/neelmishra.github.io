    Feed-Forward Networks (MLPs): Overview & Reading Guide

    October 2026 · 5 min read

    A feed-forward neural network—or Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)—is a directed acyclic composition of affine matrix projections and coordinate-wise nonlinearities. It is called feed-forward because information flows strictly from layer $\ell-1$ to layer $\ell$ without feedback loops. Before deriving backpropagation, this subseries establishes the exact indexing conventions, mini-batch GEMM shapes, polytope geometry, and approximation theorems of the forward pass.

    1. The Feed-Forward Layer Recurrence

    Starting from $a^{(0)} = x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$, each layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L\}$ applies an affine transformation $\bigl(W^{(\ell)}, b^{(\ell)}\bigr)$ followed by a point-wise activation $\phi_\ell$:

    $$z^{(\ell)} = W^{(\ell)} a^{(\ell-1)} + b^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}, \qquad a^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}, \qquad \hat{y} = a^{(L)}.$$

    2. Chapters in this Subseries

    01
    Layer Equations, Index Notation, and Mini-Batch Tensor Shapes

    Translate seamlessly between scalar index sums $z_i^{(\ell)} = \sum_{j=1}^{d_{\ell-1}} W_{ij}^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)} + b_i^{(\ell)}$, column-vector equations $W^{(\ell)} a^{(\ell-1)} + b^{(\ell)}$, and row-major mini-batch GEMMs $Z^{(\ell)} = A^{(\ell-1)}(W^{(\ell)})^\top + \mathbf{1}_B (b^{(\ell)})^\top$. Derive exact parameter counts, forward FLOPs, and work through a hand-checked $3 \to 2 \to 2$ mini-batch trace.

    02
    Polytope Geometry, Universal Approximation, and Width vs. Depth

    Derive how ReLU hyperplane arrangements partition $\mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ into convex polytopes on which $f_\theta(x) = A_R x + c_R$ is locally affine, compare Zaslavsky’s shallow bound $\sum_{j=0}^{d_0}\binom{d_1}{j}$ with Montúfar’s exponential deep folding bound, state Cybenko’s and Leshno’s Universal Approximation Theorems alongside Barron’s $O(1/N)$ Fourier rate, and prove Telgarsky’s depth-separation theorem via iterated sawtooth waves $T^{\circ L}(x)$.
