    Feed-Forward Networks (MLPs): Overview & Reading Guide

    October 2026 · 3 min read

    In a feed-forward network (MLP), signals flow one way from input to output without loops. Each layer multiplies its input by a weight matrix, adds a bias, and applies an activation function.

    1. The Feed-Forward Layer Recurrence

    Starting from $a^{(0)} = x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$, each layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L\}$ computes:

    $$z^{(\ell)} = W^{(\ell)} a^{(\ell-1)} + b^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}, \qquad a^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}, \qquad \hat{y} = a^{(L)}.$$
