    Neural Networks: Foundations, Feed-Forward Pass, and Backpropagation

    October 2026 · 4 min read

    A neural network stacks layers of linear weights and nonlinear activations. Training alternates two steps: a feed-forward pass that computes predictions and caches intermediate states, and a backpropagation pass that applies the chain rule in reverse to compute every weight’s gradient in one sweep.

    1. Anatomy of a Multilayer Neural Network

    An $L$-layer network takes inputs $a^{(0)} = x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$, passes them through hidden layers $a^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\bigl(W^{(\ell)}a^{(\ell-1)} + b^{(\ell)}\bigr)$, and produces predictions $\hat{y} = a^{(L)}$ scored by a scalar loss $\mathcal{L}(\hat{y}, y)$.
