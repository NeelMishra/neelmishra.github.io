    2. The Four Fundamental Equations of Backpropagation: Index-by-Index Scalar Derivation

    October 2026 · 17 min read

    When deriving matrix gradients, guessing where transposes $(\cdot)^\top$ and outer products go by "matching dimensions" is error-prone and hides the calculus. The cleanest way to derive backpropagation with zero ambiguity is to write the forward layer in scalar coordinates with explicit summation indices, apply the single-variable and multivariable chain rules step by step, and then recognize the resulting index sums as standard vector-matrix operations.

    1. Forward equations and error sensitivity $\delta_i^{(\ell)}$

    For an $L$-layer feed-forward network evaluated on a single input $a^{(0)} = x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ with scalar loss $\mathcal{L}\bigl(a^{(L)}, y\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}$, each neuron $i \in \{1, \dots, d_\ell\}$ in layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L\}$ computes:

    $$z_i^{(\ell)} = \sum_{j=1}^{d_{\ell-1}} W_{ij}^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)} + b_i^{(\ell)}, \qquad a_i^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\!\bigl(z_i^{(\ell)}\bigr).$$

    Because every parameter $W_{ij}^{(\ell)}$ and $b_i^{(\ell)}$ influences $\mathcal{L}$ exclusively through the pre-activation scalar $z_i^{(\ell)}$, we define the error sensitivity (adjoint) of unit $i$ in layer $\ell$ as:

    $$\boxed{\delta_i^{(\ell)} \triangleq \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}, \qquad \delta^{(\ell)} \triangleq \nabla_{z^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \begin{bmatrix} \delta_1^{(\ell)} & \cdots & \delta_{d_\ell}^{(\ell)} \end{bmatrix}^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.}$$

    2. Derivation of BP1: Output-layer sensitivity $\delta^{(L)}$

    At the output layer $\ell = L$, if $\phi_L$ is an element-wise activation function, $z_i^{(L)}$ affects $\mathcal{L}$ only through $a_i^{(L)} = \phi_L\bigl(z_i^{(L)}\bigr)$ (since $\frac{\partial a_k^{(L)}}{\partial z_i^{(L)}} = 0$ for $k \neq i$). By the single-variable chain rule:

    $$\boxed{\text{(BP1)} \qquad \delta_i^{(L)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_i^{(L)}} \cdot \frac{\partial a_i^{(L)}}{\partial z_i^{(L)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_i^{(L)}} \, \phi_L'\!\bigl(z_i^{(L)}\bigr) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \delta^{(L)} = \nabla_{a^{(L)}} \mathcal{L} \odot \phi_L'\!\bigl(z^{(L)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_L}.}$$

    (When $\phi_L = \operatorname{Softmax}$ couples all $d_L$ outputs, $\delta_i^{(L)} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_L} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_k^{(L)}} \frac{\partial a_k^{(L)}}{\partial z_i^{(L)}} = \hat{p}_i - y_i$, derived in full in Chapter 4.)

    3. Derivation of BP2: Hidden-layer sensitivity recursion $\delta^{(\ell)}$ from $\delta^{(\ell+1)}$

    Fix a hidden layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L-1\}$ and neuron $j \in \{1, \dots, d_\ell\}$. Assume $\delta_k^{(\ell+1)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}$ is already known for every neuron $k \in \{1, \dots, d_{\ell+1}\}$ in the next layer $\ell+1$. How does $z_j^{(\ell)}$ influence $\mathcal{L}$?

    1. First, $z_j^{(\ell)}$ passes through $\phi_\ell$ to produce $a_j^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr)$.
    2. Second, $a_j^{(\ell)}$ fans out across all $d_{\ell+1}$ neurons in layer $\ell+1$: $$z_k^{(\ell+1)} = \sum_{m=1}^{d_\ell} W_{km}^{(\ell+1)}\, a_m^{(\ell)} + b_k^{(\ell+1)}, \qquad k = 1, \dots, d_{\ell+1}.$$

    Writing out both steps of the chain rule explicitly:

    1. Step 2a (Derivative with respect to $a_j^{(\ell)}$): Summing over all children $z_1^{(\ell+1)}, \dots, z_{d_{\ell+1}}^{(\ell+1)}$ and using $\frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)}$: $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}} \, \frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \delta_k^{(\ell+1)} \, W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \Bigl[\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top\Bigr]_{jk} \delta_k^{(\ell+1)} = \Bigl[\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr]_j.$$
    2. Step 2b (Chain rule through $\phi_\ell$ to $z_j^{(\ell)}$): Multiplying by $\frac{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}}{\partial z_j^{(\ell)}} = \phi_\ell'\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr)$ gives BP2:
    $$\boxed{\text{(BP2)} \qquad \delta_j^{(\ell)} = \left(\sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)} \, \delta_k^{(\ell+1)}\right) \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \delta^{(\ell)} = \Bigl(\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr) \odot \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.}$$

    4. Derivation of BP3: Bias gradient $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}}$

    The bias $b_i^{(\ell)}$ appears only in $z_i^{(\ell)} = \sum_{j=1}^{d_{\ell-1}} W_{ij}^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)} + b_i^{(\ell)}$ with local derivative $\frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = 1$. Therefore:

    $$\boxed{\text{(BP3)} \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}} \cdot \frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)} \cdot 1 = \delta_i^{(\ell)} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.}$$

    5. Derivation of BP4: Weight gradient $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}}$

    The scalar weight $W_{ij}^{(\ell)}$ appears only in the $i$-th pre-activation $z_i^{(\ell)} = \dots + W_{ij}^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)} + \dots$, so $\frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = a_j^{(\ell-1)}$ (and $\frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = 0$ for $k \neq i$). Applying the chain rule through $z_i^{(\ell)}$:

    $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}} \cdot \frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)}.$$

    Since the $(i, j)$ entry of the outer product $u v^\top$ of two column vectors is $u_i v_j$, assembling all entries $(i, j)$ gives the rank-1 outer product $\delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top$:

    $$\boxed{\text{(BP4)} \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell \times d_{\ell-1}}.}$$

    6. Summary of BP1–BP4 (Single Sample)

    Equation Role Scalar / Index Form Vector / Matrix Form
    BP1 Output-layer sensitivity $\delta_i^{(L)} = \dfrac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_i^{(L)}} \, \phi_L'\!\bigl(z_i^{(L)}\bigr)$ $\delta^{(L)} = \nabla_{a^{(L)}} \mathcal{L} \odot \phi_L'\!\bigl(z^{(L)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_L}$
    BP2 Hidden-layer recursion ($\ell = L-1 \downarrow 1$) $\delta_j^{(\ell)} = \left(\sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)} \delta_k^{(\ell+1)}\right) \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr)$ $\delta^{(\ell)} = \Bigl(\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr) \odot \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}$
    BP3 Bias vector gradient $\dfrac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)}$ $\nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}$
    BP4 Weight matrix gradient $\dfrac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)}$ $\nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell \times d_{\ell-1}}$
