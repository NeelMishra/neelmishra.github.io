    1. Deriving Backpropagation (BP1–BP4): Chain Rule, Scalar Indices & Matrix Form

    October 2026 · 8 min read

    Write one layer with explicit summation indices, apply the chain rule step by step, and the four equations of backpropagation (BP1–BP4)—including every matrix transpose and outer product—fall out directly.

    1. Forward equations, the chain rule, and error sensitivity $\delta_i^{(\ell)}$

    Whenever a variable $u$ feeds into multiple downstream variables $z_1, \dots, z_m$ in the forward pass, the multivariable chain rule states that the total derivative of the scalar loss $\mathcal{L}$ with respect to $u$ sums over all those outgoing paths:

    $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial u} = \sum_{k=1}^m \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_k} \, \frac{\partial z_k}{\partial u}.$$

    In an $L$-layer feed-forward network evaluated on an input $a^{(0)} = x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ with scalar loss $\mathcal{L}\bigl(a^{(L)}, y\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}$, each neuron $i \in \{1, \dots, d_\ell\}$ in layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L\}$ computes:

    $$z_i^{(\ell)} = \sum_{j=1}^{d_{\ell-1}} W_{ij}^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)} + b_i^{(\ell)}, \qquad a_i^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\!\bigl(z_i^{(\ell)}\bigr).$$

    Because every weight $W_{ij}^{(\ell)}$ and bias $b_i^{(\ell)}$ in row $i$ influences the loss $\mathcal{L}$ exclusively through the scalar pre-activation $z_i^{(\ell)}$, we define the error sensitivity of neuron $i$ in layer $\ell$ as:

    $$\delta_i^{(\ell)} \triangleq \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}, \qquad \delta^{(\ell)} \triangleq \nabla_{z^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \begin{bmatrix} \delta_1^{(\ell)} & \cdots & \delta_{d_\ell}^{(\ell)} \end{bmatrix}^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.$$

    2. Derivation of BP1: Output-layer sensitivity $\delta^{(L)}$

    At the final layer $\ell = L$, if $\phi_L$ is an element-wise activation function, $z_i^{(L)}$ affects $\mathcal{L}$ only through $a_i^{(L)} = \phi_L\bigl(z_i^{(L)}\bigr)$. By the single-variable chain rule:

    $$\text{(BP1)} \qquad \delta_i^{(L)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_i^{(L)}} \cdot \frac{\partial a_i^{(L)}}{\partial z_i^{(L)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_i^{(L)}} \, \phi_L'\!\bigl(z_i^{(L)}\bigr) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \delta^{(L)} = \nabla_{a^{(L)}} \mathcal{L} \odot \phi_L'\!\bigl(z^{(L)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_L}.$$

    (In Chapter 2 we show that for Linear+MSE, Sigmoid+BCE, and Softmax+Cross-Entropy, this starting vector simplifies to $\delta^{(L)} = \hat{y} - y$.)

    3. Derivation of BP2: Hidden-layer sensitivity recursion $\delta^{(\ell)}$ from $\delta^{(\ell+1)}$

    Fix a hidden layer $\ell \in \{1, \dots, L-1\}$ and neuron $j \in \{1, \dots, d_\ell\}$. Suppose $\delta_k^{(\ell+1)} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}$ is already known for every neuron $k \in \{1, \dots, d_{\ell+1}\}$ in the next layer $\ell+1$. How does $z_j^{(\ell)}$ influence $\mathcal{L}$?

    1. First, $z_j^{(\ell)}$ passes through $\phi_\ell$ to produce $a_j^{(\ell)} = \phi_\ell\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr)$.
    2. Second, $a_j^{(\ell)}$ fans out across all $d_{\ell+1}$ neurons in layer $\ell+1$:
      $$z_k^{(\ell+1)} = \sum_{m=1}^{d_\ell} W_{km}^{(\ell+1)}\, a_m^{(\ell)} + b_k^{(\ell+1)}, \qquad k = 1, \dots, d_{\ell+1}.$$

    Writing out both steps of the chain rule explicitly:

    1. Step 2a (Derivative with respect to $a_j^{(\ell)}$): Summing over all children $z_1^{(\ell+1)}, \dots, z_{d_{\ell+1}}^{(\ell+1)}$ and using $\frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)}$:
      $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}} \, \frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell+1)}}{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \delta_k^{(\ell+1)} \, W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)} = \sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} \Bigl[\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top\Bigr]_{jk} \delta_k^{(\ell+1)} = \Bigl[\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr]_j.$$
    2. Step 2b (Chain rule through $\phi_\ell$ to $z_j^{(\ell)}$): Multiplying by $\frac{\partial a_j^{(\ell)}}{\partial z_j^{(\ell)}} = \phi_\ell'\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr)$ gives BP2:
    $$\text{(BP2)} \qquad \delta_j^{(\ell)} = \left(\sum_{k=1}^{d_{\ell+1}} W_{kj}^{(\ell+1)} \, \delta_k^{(\ell+1)}\right) \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z_j^{(\ell)}\bigr) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \delta^{(\ell)} = \Bigl(\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr) \odot \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.$$

    4. Derivation of BP3: Bias gradient $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}}$

    The bias $b_i^{(\ell)}$ appears only in $z_i^{(\ell)} = \sum_{j=1}^{d_{\ell-1}} W_{ij}^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)} + b_i^{(\ell)}$ with local derivative $\frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = 1$. Therefore:

    $$\text{(BP3)} \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}} \cdot \frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial b_i^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)} \cdot 1 = \delta_i^{(\ell)} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell}.$$

    5. Derivation of BP4: Weight gradient $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}}$

    The scalar weight $W_{ij}^{(\ell)}$ appears only in the $i$-th pre-activation $z_i^{(\ell)} = \dots + W_{ij}^{(\ell)} a_j^{(\ell-1)} + \dots$, so $\frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = a_j^{(\ell-1)}$ (and $\frac{\partial z_k^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = 0$ for $k \neq i$). Applying the chain rule through $z_i^{(\ell)}$:

    $$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}} \cdot \frac{\partial z_i^{(\ell)}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)}.$$

    Since the $(i, j)$ entry of the outer product $u v^\top$ of two column vectors is $u_i v_j$, assembling all entries $(i, j)$ gives the rank-1 outer product $\delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top$:

    $$\text{(BP4)} \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial W_{ij}^{(\ell)}} = \delta_i^{(\ell)}\, a_j^{(\ell-1)} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\ell \times d_{\ell-1}}.$$

    6. Mini-Batch Matrix Form: Stacking $B$ Samples into 2 Backward GEMMs

    In practice we train on a mini-batch of $B$ examples stacked as rows: $A^{(\ell-1)} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d_{\ell-1}}$ and $Z^{(\ell)} = A^{(\ell-1)}(W^{(\ell)})^\top + \mathbf{1}_B(b^{(\ell)})^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d_\ell}$. Let $G^{(\ell)} \triangleq \nabla_{Z^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d_\ell}$ stack the $B$ row sensitivities $\frac{1}{B}(\delta^{(b,\ell)})^\top$. Then BP2, BP3, and BP4 across all $B$ samples become standard matrix operations:

    $$\begin{aligned} G^{(\ell-1)} &= \bigl(G^{(\ell)} W^{(\ell)}\bigr) \odot \phi_{\ell-1}'\!\bigl(Z^{(\ell-1)}\bigr), \\ \nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} &= \sum_{b=1}^B \bigl(G_{b,:}^{(\ell)}\bigr)^\top A_{b,:}^{(\ell-1)} = \bigl(G^{(\ell)}\bigr)^\top A^{(\ell-1)}, \qquad \nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \bigl(G^{(\ell)}\bigr)^\top \mathbf{1}_B. \end{aligned}$$

    7. Summary of BP1–BP4 (Single Sample & Mini-Batch)

    Equation Role Single-Sample Vector Form Mini-Batch Matrix Form ($B$ rows)
    BP1 Output-layer sensitivity $\delta^{(L)} = \nabla_{a^{(L)}} \mathcal{L} \odot \phi_L'\!\bigl(z^{(L)}\bigr)$ $G^{(L)} = \nabla_{A^{(L)}} \mathcal{L} \odot \phi_L'\!\bigl(Z^{(L)}\bigr)$
    BP2 Hidden-layer recursion ($\ell = L-1 \downarrow 1$) $\delta^{(\ell)} = \Bigl(\bigl(W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr)^\top \delta^{(\ell+1)}\Bigr) \odot \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(z^{(\ell)}\bigr)$ $G^{(\ell)} = \bigl(G^{(\ell+1)} W^{(\ell+1)}\bigr) \odot \phi_\ell'\!\bigl(Z^{(\ell)}\bigr)$
    BP3 Bias vector gradient $\nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)}$ $\nabla_{b^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \bigl(G^{(\ell)}\bigr)^\top \mathbf{1}_B$
    BP4 Weight matrix gradient $\nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \delta^{(\ell)} \bigl(a^{(\ell-1)}\bigr)^\top$ $\nabla_{W^{(\ell)}} \mathcal{L} = \bigl(G^{(\ell)}\bigr)^\top A^{(\ell-1)}$
