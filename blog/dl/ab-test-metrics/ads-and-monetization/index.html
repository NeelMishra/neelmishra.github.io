    Subdomain 02 · Digital Advertising, Click Prediction & Auction Monetization

    October 2026 · 5 Deep-Dive Notes · Ad Ranking & Auction Systems

    Ad Click-Through Rate ($\text{pCTR}$) and Conversion Rate ($\text{pCVR}$) prediction are the most frequently asked ML System Design interview topics at Meta, Google, Amazon, TikTok, and Pinterest. Unlike organic recommendation, an ad ranking model feeds predicted probabilities directly into a Generalized Second-Price ($\text{GSP}$) or VCG auction where probabilities determine real dollar clearing prices. Consequently, online A/B tests in ads must track position-debiased click rates ($\text{COEC}$), delayed post-click conversions, auction revenue ($\text{eCPM}/\text{RPM}$), causal advertiser incrementality ($\text{iROAS}$), and strict probability calibration ($\text{COPC}$).

    Quick Formula Reference

    Metric Mathematical Definition Primary Role in Ad Systems
    Position-Normalized COEC $\text{COEC} = \frac{\sum_{k} C_k}{\sum_{k} I_k \cdot \overline{\text{CTR}}_k}$ Measures intrinsic ad relevance lift adjusted for slot/position mix changes
    Effective CPM (eCPM) $\text{eCPM}_a = 1000 \cdot \hat{p}(\text{click}\mid u,a) \cdot \text{CPC\_Bid}_a$ Common currency ranking oCPM, CPC, and CPM ads in a unified auction
    GSP Clearing Price ($\text{CPC}_k$) $\text{CPC}_k = \frac{\hat{p}_{k+1} \cdot b_{k+1} + (\tau_{\text{quality}, k+1} - \tau_{\text{quality}, k})}{\hat{p}_k} + \$0.01$ Determines actual revenue charged per click in Generalized Second-Price auctions
    Incremental ROAS ($\text{iROAS}$) $\text{iROAS} = \frac{\bar{V}_{\text{treated}}^{\text{ghost-matched}} - \bar{V}_{\text{control}}^{\text{ghost-ad}}}{\text{Ad Spend}_{\text{treated}}}$ Causal advertiser revenue generated per $\$1$ of ad spend above organic baseline
    Calibration ($\text{COPC}$) & $\text{NE}$ $\text{COPC} = \frac{\sum_i y_i}{\sum_i \hat{p}_i}, \quad \text{NE} = \frac{\text{LogLoss}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}, \mathbf{y})}{- \bar{y}\ln\bar{y} - (1-\bar{y})\ln(1-\bar{y})}$ Ensures predicted probabilities match true rates across auction slices
