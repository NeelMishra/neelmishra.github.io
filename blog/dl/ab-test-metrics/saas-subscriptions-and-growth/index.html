    SaaS, Subscriptions, FinTech & Growth ML Systems: A/B Test Metrics Reading Guide

    October 2026 · Subdomain 06 · 3 Deep-Dive Notes

    In subscription products (Netflix, Spotify, Duolingo, ChatGPT Plus, B2B SaaS, and FinTech neobanks), value accrues over months or years of recurring renewals rather than in a single session click. Experimenting on subscription, promo-targeting, and push-notification ML systems requires survival analysis for right-censored churn data, discounted Customer Lifetime Value ($\text{LTV}$) surrogates, causal uplift metrics (Qini / $\text{AUUC}$) that separate Persuadables from Sleeping Dogs, and Lagrangian fatigue guardrails that price every push notification against permanent permission revocation.

    Deep-Dive Notes in Subdomain 06

    6.1
    Logo Churn, Net Dollar Retention (NDR) & Kaplan–Meier Survival Hazard Rates

    Distinguish Logo Churn, Gross Dollar Churn, and Net Dollar Retention ($\text{NDR}$); separate Voluntary (cancellation) from Involuntary (payment dunning/card failure) churn; and evaluate right-censored A/B test cohorts using the Kaplan–Meier survival curve $\hat{S}(t)$, discrete hazard rate $h_t$, Log-Rank test, and Restricted Mean Survival Time ($\text{RMST}$).

    6.2
    LTV, ARPU, LTV:CAC Payback Period & Qini / AUUC Incrementality Uplift

    Derive Discounted Customer Lifetime Value ($\text{LTV} = \sum_{t=0}^\infty \frac{M_t S(t)}{(1+d)^t} = \frac{\text{ARPU}\cdot\text{Margin}}{c + d}$), $\text{LTV}:\text{CAC}$ ratio, and CAC Payback Period; then master Causal Uplift Modeling ($\tau(x) = P(Y=1\mid X=x, T=1) - P(Y=1\mid X=x, T=0)$), the four user response quadrants, and the Qini / $\text{AUUC}$ evaluation curves.

    6.3
    Push & Email Notification CTR, Volume Efficiency & Unsubscribe / Fatigue Guardrails

    See why raw notification Open/Click Rate ($\text{CTR}$) rewards spam; measure Send Volume Efficiency ($\text{Incremental DAU per } 1{,}000\text{ Sends}$) via persistent notification holdouts; and formulate the constrained optimization dual $\text{Score}(u, m) = \hat{\tau}_{\text{DAU}}(u, m) - \lambda\,\hat{p}_{\text{unsub}}(u, m)$ to bound OS permission revocations and app uninstalls.

    Metric Formula Primary Blind Spot if Misused Production Best Practice
    Net Dollar Retention ($\text{NDR}$) $\dfrac{\text{ARR}_0 + \text{Exp} - \text{Contr} - \text{Churn}}{\text{ARR}_0}$ Large enterprise seat expansion can mask high SMB customer logo churn Always report Logo Churn, Gross Dollar Retention ($\text{GDR} \le 100\%$), and $\text{NDR}$ together
    Restricted Mean Survival Time ($\text{RMST}(\tau)$) $\int_0^\tau \hat{S}(t)\,dt$ Naïve binary 30-day churn ignores whether a user churned on Day 2 vs. Day 29 and drops staggered cohorts Use Kaplan–Meier $\text{RMST}(\tau)$ and Log-Rank test to incorporate right-censored users seamlessly
    Discounted $\text{LTV}$ $\sum_{t=0}^\infty \dfrac{M_t S(t)}{(1+d)^t}$ Constant-hazard formula $\frac{M}{c+d}$ overestimates $\text{LTV}$ by $30\%\text{–}60\%$ because true hazard $h_t$ decreases with tenure Fit a Weibull / Beta-Geometric survival curve or multi-horizon surrogate index ($D_7, D_{30}$ engagement + auto-renew state)
    Qini / $\text{AUUC}$ (Uplift) $\int_0^1 \bigl(U(q) - q\,U(1)\bigr)\,dq$ Targeting retention discounts by raw $\hat{P}(\text{churn})$ subsidizes Sure Things and wakes up Sleeping Dogs Rank users by net incremental profit $\hat{\tau}_{\text{retain}}(x)\cdot\text{LTV} - P(\text{accept}\mid T=1)\cdot\text{Discount}$
    Notification Volume Efficiency $\dfrac{\text{DAU}_T - \text{DAU}_C}{(N_{\text{sends}, T} - N_{\text{sends}, C})/1000}$ Sending $2\times$ more pushes increases total clicks while destroying marginal lift per push and spiking unsubscribes Gate sends with dual shadow price $\lambda$ tuned to keep OS permission revocation $\le \epsilon_{\max}$
