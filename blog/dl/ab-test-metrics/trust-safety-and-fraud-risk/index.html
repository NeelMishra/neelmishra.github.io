    Trust, Safety, Content Moderation & Fraud Risk: A/B Test Metrics Reading Guide

    October 2026 · Subdomain 08 · 2 Deep-Dive Notes

    Fraud detection (Stripe, PayPal, Adyen, e-commerce checkout risk) and Trust & Safety content moderation (YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Trust & Safety classifiers) share a fundamental statistical challenge: selective labels / counterfactual censoring. Whenever your model blocks a payment transaction or takes down a piece of content, you censor what would have happened if it had been allowed through! Worse, ground-truth chargebacks take $30\text{–}90\text{ days}$ to arrive from card-issuing banks. This subdomain covers how production fraud and moderation systems use random exploration pass-through slices, Horvitz–Thompson stratified audit sampling, Net Risk-Adjusted Margin, and Violative View Rate ($\text{VVR}$) to run rigorous online A/B tests.

    Deep-Dive Notes in Subdomain 08

    8.1
    Payment Chargeback Rate (bps), False Decline (Customer Insult Rate) & Net Risk-Adjusted Margin

    Define count-based and dollar-weighted Chargeback Rate in basis points ($\text{bps}$) under 30–90 day dispute maturation lag; estimate good-user False Decline (Customer Insult Rate) using a randomized Exploration Pass-Through Slice ($\epsilon \approx 1\%$) and Horvitz–Thompson weighting; and optimize Net Risk-Adjusted Contribution Margin accounting for principal loss, $\$15$ network dispute fees, and post-insult LTV churn.

    8.2
    Violative View Rate (VVR), Proactive Automated Catch Rate & Appeal Overturn Guardrails

    Understand why "Total Violative Posts Removed" is a broken vanity metric; derive Violative View Rate ($\text{VVR} = \sum_{i} v_i y_i / \sum_{i} v_i$) and stratified Horvitz–Thompson human audit variance; and pair Proactive Automated Catch Rate (before any user flag) with User Appeal Rate, Appeal Overturn Rate (Precision guardrail), and Moderator Queue $p_{95}$ Latency.

    Fraud & Trust/Safety Metric Summary Table

    Metric Exact Formula Selective-Label / Censoring Challenge Production Estimator / Guardrail
    Dollar Chargeback Rate ($\text{CBR}_{\$}$) $\dfrac{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}} d_i\,C_i}{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}} d_i}\times 10^4\text{ bps}$ 30–90 day issuer dispute lag; card networks (Visa/Mastercard) levy fines if $\text{CBR}_{\text{count}} > 75\text{–}90\text{ bps}$ Use 7-day issuer early-fraud-warning (TC40/SAFE) + 3DS challenge failure surrogates during 14-day A/B tests
    False Decline / Customer Insult Rate $\Pr(\text{Legitimate} \mid \text{Declined by Model})$ Blocked transactions never reach the bank, so true legitimacy $Y_i \in \{0,1\}$ is unobserved on blocked traffic Pass through a random $\epsilon \approx 1\%$ exploration slice of model-declined transactions (below extreme risk cap)
    Net Risk-Adjusted Margin $\sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}} \bigl(r d_i - (d_i + f_{\text{cb}})C_i\bigr) - \sum_{j \in \mathcal{D}} \hat{p}_{\text{good}, j} L_{\text{insult}, j}$ Lowering approval threshold cuts fraud bps at the cost of blocking high-LTV repeat customers Set decision threshold $\tau^*$ where marginal take-rate profit equals marginal expected fraud + insult loss
    Violative View Rate ($\text{VVR}$) $\dfrac{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{U}} v_i\,y_i}{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{U}} v_i}$ Impossible to have human raters label all $10^9$ daily impressions ($v_i$ views on item $i$) Stratified PPS / Horvitz–Thompson random audit sampling weighted by impression volume and classifier score strata
    Appeal Overturn Rate ($\text{AOR}$) $\dfrac{N_{\text{appeals overturned by human}}}{N_{\text{appealed enforcement actions}}}$ Lowering classifier threshold boosts Proactive Catch Rate while wrongly banning innocent creators Enforce hard upper-bound guardrail on $\text{AOR}$ and audit unappealed takedowns for false positives
