    Subdomain 01 · Statistical Foundations, Variance Reduction & Guardrails

    October 2026 · 5 Deep-Dive Notes · Experimentation Infrastructure

    Before evaluating whether a ranking model improves $\text{CTR}$, $\text{GMV}$, or Watch Time, an ML engineer must design a statistically valid online experiment. How do you combine competing objectives into a single Overall Evaluation Criterion ($\text{OEC}$)? How many users are needed to detect a $+0.5\%$ lift with $80\%$ power? Why does the standard $t$-test fail when randomization is by user but the metric is a ratio over impressions—and how do the Delta Method and CUPED rescue experiment sensitivity?

    OEC, North Star Decomposition & Guardrail Metrics

    01.2
    Hypothesis Testing, Statistical Power ($1-\beta$), MDE & Sample Size Formulas

    Deriving the fundamental sample size formula $n = \frac{2\sigma^2(z_{1-\alpha/2} + z_{1-\beta})^2}{\delta^2}$, Minimum Detectable Effect ($\text{MDE}$), peeking inflation, Always-Valid $p$-values (mSPRT), and multiple-testing corrections (Bonferroni vs. Benjamini–Hochberg FDR).

    01.3
    Ratio Metrics, Intra-User Clustering & the Delta Method Linearization

    Why naïve Bernoulli variance $\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{N_{\text{imp}}}$ underestimates true variance by $3\times\text{–}10\times$ when users generate multiple impressions. Full first-order Taylor expansion of $\text{Var}(\bar{Y}/\bar{X})$ and per-user linearized metric $L_u = Y_u - R \cdot X_u$.

    01.4
    CUPED & CUPAC: Pre-Experiment Covariate Variance Reduction ($1 - \rho^2$)

    Controlled-experiment Using Pre-Experiment Data ($\text{CUPED}$): deriving the optimal control-variate coefficient $\theta^* = \text{Cov}(Y, X)/\text{Var}(X)$, proving the exact variance multiplier $(1-\rho^2)$, Equivalent Traffic Gain $1/(1-\rho^2)$, and ML-driven $\text{CUPAC}$.

    01.5
    Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), Novelty/Primacy & Network Interference

    Diagnosing broken randomization with the $\chi^2$ Sample Ratio Mismatch test, separating transient Novelty Effects from true learning via cohort slicing, and designing Switchback, Geo-Cluster, and Ego-Cluster experiments when SUTVA fails.

    Quick Formula Reference

    Concept / Metric Core Mathematical Expression ML System Design Role
    Constrained OEC $\text{OEC} = \Delta \mu_{\text{driver}} - \sum_k \lambda_k \max(0, \Delta g_k - \tau_k)$ Balances primary product utility against latency, ad load, or churn guardrails
    Two-Sample Size $n$ (per arm) $n = \frac{2\sigma^2 (z_{1-\alpha/2} + z_{1-\beta})^2}{\delta_{\text{abs}}^2} \approx \frac{16\sigma^2}{\delta_{\text{abs}}^2}$ Determines required traffic and runtime duration before launching an A/B test
    Delta Method Ratio Variance $\text{Var}\!\left(\frac{\bar{Y}}{\bar{X}}\right) \approx \frac{1}{n \mu_X^2}\left(\sigma_Y^2 - 2R\sigma_{YX} + R^2\sigma_X^2\right)$ Computes valid $p$-values for $\text{CTR}$, $\text{CVR}$, and $\text{AOV}$ when randomized by user
    CUPED Adjusted Estimator $\hat{Y}_u^{\text{cv}} = Y_u - \theta^*(X_u - \mu_X), \quad \text{Var}(\bar{Y}^{\text{cv}}) = \text{Var}(\bar{Y})(1 - \rho_{XY}^2)$ Shrinks confidence intervals by $30\%\text{–}60\%$ without needing more users
    SRM Chi-Square Test $\chi^2 = \sum_{k} \frac{(O_k - E_k)^2}{E_k} \sim \chi^2_{K-1}$ Invalidates experiments with bot filtering, redirect loss, or logging asymmetry
