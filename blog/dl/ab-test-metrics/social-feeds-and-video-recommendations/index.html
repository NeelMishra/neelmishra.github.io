    Subdomain 04: Social Feeds, Short-Form Video & Streaming Recommendations A/B Metrics

    October 2026 · Subdomain 04 Reading Guide · 3 Deep-Dive Notes

    Modern social feeds and video recommenders (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, X, Netflix) do not optimize a single objective. Every ranked impression triggers a vector of competing behavioral outcomes: passive consumption (Dwell Time, Watch Time, Completion Rate, Loops), active high-value engagement (Likes, Shares, Saves, Comments, Follows), explicit dissatisfaction (Fast-Swipes $<3\text{s}$, Hides, "Not Interested", Reports), long-term habit formation ($\text{DAU}/\text{MAU}$ Stickiness, $L_7/L_{28}$ Active Days, Cohort Retention), and two-sided Creator Ecosystem Health. This subdomain derives the exact mathematics, multi-task value models, and statistical guardrails used to evaluate production feed ranking systems.

    The Fundamental Feed Ranking Equation & Metric Tension

    In a multi-task recommendation ranker, given user $u$, candidate item (post/video) $i$ of duration $D_i$, and context $c$, the model predicts a vector of event probabilities $\hat{p}_k(u, i) = \Pr(e_k = 1 \mid u, i, c)$ and expected watch time $\hat{T}(u, i) = \mathbb{E}[T_{\text{watch}} \mid u, i, c]$. Items are ranked by a composite Value Model:

    $$V(u, i) = \sum_{k \in \mathcal{E}_+} w_k \, \hat{p}_k(u, i)^{\alpha_k} + w_{\text{watch}} \, g\bigl(\hat{T}(u, i), D_i\bigr) - \sum_{m \in \mathcal{E}_-} \lambda_m \, \hat{p}_m(u, i)$$

    Why can't we evaluate this system online with a single naive metric? Because every standalone feed metric has a fatal degenerate optimum:

    FAILURE MODE 01 Pure Watch-Time Bias ($\text{Length Bias}$) Optimizing raw $\mathbb{E}[T_{\text{watch}}]$ floods the feed with $10$-minute rambling videos and penalizes punchy $15$-second clips, reducing diversity and session frequency.
    FAILURE MODE 02 Pure Completion Rate Bias ($\text{Short-Clip Bias}$) Optimizing raw $\text{VCR} = T_{\text{watch}} / D_i$ floods the feed with $5$-second looping meme clips that finish before the user can swipe away.
    FAILURE MODE 03 Outrage / Clickbait Engagement Traps Polarizing or sensational posts generate high comment and click rates ($\hat{p}_{\text{comment}}\uparrow$) while driving up Hide/Report rates and eroding $D_{28}$ retention.

    Measure long-term habit formation via $\text{DAU}/\text{MAU}$ Stickiness, analyze the bimodal $L_7$ and $L_{28}$ Power-User Curve, decompose Total Watch Time into Session Frequency $\times$ Mean Session Duration, formulate $D_1, D_7, D_{28}$ Cohort Retention, and construct high-power Short-Term Surrogate Index Metrics to predict 90-day retention inside a 14-day A/B test.

    03
    Explicit Engagement Value Models, Negative Feedback Guardrails & Creator Ecosystem Fairness

    Calibrate the multi-task weights $w_k$ for Likes, Shares, Saves, Comments, and Follows via retention marginal utility; enforce strict guardrails on Hide, "Not Interested", Dislike, and Report Rates; and evaluate two-sided Creator Ecosystem Health via Creator Publishing Retention, Cold-Start First-$100$-Views Rate, and the Exposure Gini Coefficient.

    Social Feeds & Video Recommendations Online Metrics Reference Matrix

    Metric Name Mathematical Definition Healthy Direction Primary Blind Spot / Guardrail Pair
    Daily Watch Time / User $\dfrac{1}{N}\sum_{u=1}^N \sum_{d=1}^{D} \min(T_{u,d}, \tau_{\text{cap}})$ Higher ($\uparrow$) Inherently favors longer videos; always pair with Completed Videos / DAU and Session Frequency.
    Video Completion Rate ($\text{VCR}$) $\dfrac{1}{|\mathcal{I}|}\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}}\min\!\left(\dfrac{T_{\text{watch},i}}{D_i},\, c_{\max}\right)$ Higher ($\uparrow$) Severely biased toward short $5\text{s}\text{–}10\text{s}$ videos; replace raw $\text{VCR}$ with Duration-Bucket Quantile $q\text{-VCR}$.
    Duration-Quantile Watch ($q\text{-VCR}$) $F_{T \mid D_i \in B_b}(T_{\text{watch},i}) \in [0, 1]$ Higher ($\uparrow$) Measures the empirical percentile of watch time among videos in the same duration bucket $B_b$; duration-invariant!
    Skip-Before-$3\text{s}$ Rate $\dfrac{1}{|\mathcal{I}_{\text{video}}|}\sum_{i}\mathbb{I}[T_{\text{watch},i} < 3.0\text{s}]$ Lower ($\downarrow$) Fast-swipe bounce signal indicating poor initial hook or mismatched topic recommendation.
    $L_7$ Active Days / User $\dfrac{1}{N}\sum_{u=1}^N \sum_{t=1}^7 \mathbb{I}[\text{Active}_{u,t}]$ Higher ($\uparrow$) More sensitive in a 2-week A/B test than binary $D_7$ retention; apply CUPED using pre-experiment $L_{7,\text{pre}}$.
    Composite Explicit Value / DAU $\dfrac{1}{N}\sum_{u=1}^N \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_u}\sum_{k \in \mathcal{E}_+} w_k e_{u,i,k}$ Higher ($\uparrow$) Weights $w_{\text{share}} > w_{\text{save}} > w_{\text{comment}} > w_{\text{like}}$ reflect downstream retention & viral loop value.
    Negative Feedback Rate ($\text{NFR}$) $\dfrac{\sum_{u,i}\mathbb{I}[\text{Hide}_{u,i} \lor \text{NotInt}_{u,i} \lor \text{Report}_{u,i}]}{\sum_u |\mathcal{I}_u|}$ Lower ($\downarrow$) Measured in basis points ($\text{bps}$); must not regress even if Watch Time and Comments increase.
    Creator Exposure Gini ($G$) $\dfrac{\sum_{c=1}^M (2c - M - 1) E_{(c)}}{M \sum_{c=1}^M E_{(c)}} \in [0, 1]$ Lower ($\downarrow$) Detects Superstar / Matthew-effect concentration where top $1\%$ of creators capture $95\%$ of impressions.
