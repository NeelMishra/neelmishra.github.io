    E-Commerce, Ride-Sharing & Two-Sided Marketplaces: A/B Test Metrics Reading Guide

    October 2026 · Subdomain 05 · 3 Deep-Dive Notes

    Unlike pure attention platforms where an impression or click is the terminal product, e-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Shopify, Zalando) and real-time physical-world marketplaces (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Airbnb) transact real dollars and constrained physical inventory. Optimizing a recommendation or ranking model for raw clicks or gross checkout volume routinely backfires—either by suppressing affordable high-conversion items, recommending sizing-ambiguous apparel with $40\%$ return rates, or cannibalizing nearby driver/courier supply. This subdomain covers the mathematical definitions, variance properties, and guardrail hierarchies for transactional and two-sided marketplaces.

    Deep-Dive Notes in Subdomain 05

    5.1
    GMV, Revenue Per Visitor (RPV), Average Order Value (AOV) & Net Take Rate

    Decompose Gross Merchandise Value ($\text{GMV}$) and Revenue Per Visitor ($\text{RPV} = \text{CVR} \times \text{AOV}$); prove why $\text{AOV}$ is a post-treatment conditioned ratio metric that can increase when a model destroys cheap conversions; derive Delta Method variance and $p_{99.5}$ whale-buyer winsorization for heavy-tailed revenue; and formulate Net Take Rate and Contribution Margin.

    5.2
    Multi-Stage Funnel Conversion, Cart Abandonment & Return-Adjusted Net GMV

    Track micro-conversion progression across Impression $\to$ PDP Click $\to$ Add-to-Cart ($\text{ATC}$) $\to$ Checkout Initiation $\to$ Order Completion; diagnose Cart Abandonment Rate ($1 - N_{\text{orders}}/N_{\text{carts}}$) spikes caused by shipping-fee shock or out-of-stock latency; and incorporate return probability $\hat{p}_{\text{return}}(u, i)$ and reverse-logistics cost $c_{\text{ret}}$ directly into the multi-task ranking score.

    5.3
    Two-Sided Marketplace Liquidity, ETA Pinball Loss & Surge-Pricing Guardrails

    Define Buyer Search-to-Fill Rate (Liquidity) and the Supply Utilization vs. Demand Wait-Time efficiency frontier; evaluate asymmetric ETA errors with Pinball Loss $\rho_\tau$ and Extreme Late Delivery Rate ($>15\text{ min}$); and pair dynamic surge pricing models with conversion elasticity and Switchback cannibalization guardrails.
