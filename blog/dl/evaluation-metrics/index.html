    Evaluation Metrics in Deep Learning: Reading Guide

    October 2026 · 6 min read

    Eight connected chapters covering the mathematics, geometry, and failure modes of evaluation metrics across every domain of machine learning and deep learning: from confusion matrices, $F_\beta$, MCC, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and Brier calibration to regression/forecasting (RMSE, $R^2$, MASE, Pinball, CRPS), search & recommendation ranking (MRR, MAP@$K$, nDCG@$K$, Novelty, Diversity), vision & clustering (IoU, Dice, COCO mAP, FID, Silhouette, ARI/NMI), explainability & fairness (Deletion/Insertion AUC, Infidelity, Sufficiency, Equalized Odds), classical NLP (Perplexity, BPB, BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore), and modern LLM/RAG evaluation (unbiased $\text{pass}@k$, RAGAS, Bradley–Terry Arena Elo, and FActScore).

    A loss function tells gradient descent how to move weights during training, but an evaluation metric defines what it actually means for a system to succeed in deployment. Choosing the wrong metric—such as raw Accuracy on a $0.1\%$-positive fraud dataset, ROC-AUC when top-$K$ precision matters, BLEU on open-ended reasoning, or naive $\text{pass}@k$ without combinatorial bias correction—can make a broken model look state-of-the-art. This series derives every standard metric from first principles, exposes its blind spots with concrete counterexamples, and implements each metric cleanly in Python and PyTorch.

    Eight chapters across every modeling paradigm

    01
    Classification Metrics: Confusion Matrix Geometry, Precision, Recall, $F_\beta$, MCC, and Cohen’s $\kappa$

    Decompose the $2\times 2$ and $C\times C$ confusion matrices; derive Precision, Recall, Specificity, Balanced Accuracy, and why $F_1$ is the harmonic mean (not arithmetic mean); prove how Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) and Cohen’s $\kappa$ account for all four quadrants; and compare Macro, Micro, and Weighted multiclass/multilabel averaging.

    02
    Ranking & Calibration Metrics: ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Log-Loss, Brier Decomposition, and ECE

    Prove the Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney identity $\text{ROC-AUC}=\Pr(\hat s_+ > \hat s_-)$, see why ROC-AUC is invariant to class prevalence while PR-AUC (Average Precision) exposes false-positive floods, derive Murphy’s 3-way Brier Score decomposition into Reliability, Resolution, and Uncertainty, and compute Expected Calibration Error (ECE).

    03
    Regression & Forecasting Metrics: MAE, RMSE, Huber, $R^2$, MAPE/MASE, Pinball Loss, and CRPS

    Prove why MSE elicits the conditional mean $\mathbb E[Y|X]$ while MAE elicits the conditional median and Pinball loss $\rho_\tau$ elicits the $\tau$-quantile; compare $R^2$ and Adjusted $R^2$; see why MAPE is asymmetric and how Hyndman’s MASE fixes zero-demand time series; and evaluate full predictive distributions with CRPS.

    04
    Search & Recommendation Ranking Metrics: Precision@$K$, Recall@$K$, MRR, MAP@$K$, nDCG@$K$, and Diversity/Novelty

    Evaluate ranked lists where top positions matter exponentially more than tail positions: work through Hit Rate@$K$, Precision@$K$, Recall@$K$, Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR), MAP@$K$, graded-relevance nDCG@$K$ with logarithmic position discounting, RBP, and beyond-accuracy recommender metrics (Coverage, Gini, Intra-List Diversity, and Self-Information Novelty).

    05
    Vision, Clustering & Generative Metrics: IoU/GIoU, Dice, COCO mAP, Panoptic Quality, FID, and Silhouette/ARI/NMI

    Connect pixel and box overlap via IoU (Jaccard), GIoU/CIoU, and Sørensen–Dice ($\text{Dice}=\frac{2\text{IoU}}{1+\text{IoU}}$); trace COCO $\text{mAP}@[.50:.05:.95]$ and Panoptic Quality ($\text{PQ}=\text{SQ}\times\text{RQ}$); derive PSNR, SSIM, and Fréchet Inception Distance (FID); and evaluate unsupervised clusters with Silhouette, Davies–Bouldin, ARI, and NMI.

    06
    Explainability (XAI) & Fairness Metrics: Deletion/Insertion AUC, Infidelity, Sufficiency, Pointing Game, and Equalized Odds

    Quantitatively evaluate feature attributions $\phi(x)$ (SHAP, Integrated Gradients, Grad-CAM): derive Deletion (MoRF) and Insertion (LeRF) curves, ERASER Comprehensiveness & Sufficiency, Yeh et al.’s Infidelity and Max-Sensitivity, Pointing Game localization, and group fairness metrics (Demographic Parity, Equal Opportunity, and Equalized Odds).

    07
    NLP & Generation Metrics: Perplexity, Bits-Per-Byte, BLEU, ROUGE, WER, and BERTScore

    Connect cross-entropy to Perplexity ($\text{PPL}=e^{H}$) and tokenizer-agnostic Bits-Per-Byte (BPB); derive BLEU’s clipped $n$-gram precision and Brevity Penalty, ROUGE-N/ROUGE-L, METEOR, chrF++, and Levenshtein Word Error Rate (WER); and move to contextual embedding alignment with BERTScore.

    08
    Modern LLM & RAG Metrics: Unbiased $\text{pass}@k$, RAGAS Faithfulness Triad, Bradley–Terry Elo, and FActScore

    Derive the unbiased combinatorial $\text{pass}@k = 1 - \binom{n-c}{k}/\binom{n}{k}$ estimator for code and reasoning; decompose RAG evaluation into Context Precision, Context Recall, Faithfulness, and Answer Relevance; model pairwise human/LLM-Judge preferences with Bradley–Terry Elo and Length-Controlled Win Rates; and measure atomic factuality with FActScore.
