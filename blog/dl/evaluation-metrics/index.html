    Evaluation Metrics in Deep Learning

    October 2026 · 4 min read

    A loss function guides gradient descent during training; an evaluation metric measures whether the model actually solves the task in deployment. Each chapter below derives the standard metrics for its domain, works through a numerical example where naive metrics fail, and provides a compact PyTorch implementation.

    Chapters

    01
    Classification: Confusion Matrix, $F_\beta$, MCC, and Cohen’s $\kappa$

    Row- vs. column-conditioned rates, why $F_1$ uses the harmonic mean, why MCC includes True Negatives, and Macro vs. Micro multiclass averaging.

     02
    Ranking & Calibration: ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Brier Score, and ECE

    The Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney probability view of ROC-AUC, why PR-AUC exposes rare-class false positives, Brier decomposition, and Expected Calibration Error.

     03
    Regression & Forecasting: MAE, RMSE, $R^2$, MASE, Pinball, and CRPS

    Why MSE predicts the mean while MAE predicts the median, why MAPE fails near zero, and how Pinball loss and CRPS evaluate uncertainty.

     04
    Search & Recommendations: Precision@$K$, MRR, MAP@$K$, and nDCG@$K$

    Top-$K$ cutoffs, reciprocal rank for single-answer queries, Average Precision, logarithmic discount in nDCG@$K$, and catalog diversity.

     05
    Generative Vision & Clustering: Inception Score, FID, Silhouette, ARI, and NMI

    Inception Score KL divergence, Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) 2-Wasserstein alignment, and internal/external clustering scores (Silhouette, DBI, CHI, ARI, NMI).

     06
    NLP Generation: Perplexity, Bits-Per-Byte, BLEU, ROUGE, WER, and BERTScore

    Exponentiated cross-entropy (PPL), tokenizer-agnostic BPB, clipped $n$-gram BLEU with Brevity Penalty, edit-distance WER, and embedding alignment.

     07
    LLMs & RAG: Unbiased $\text{pass}@k$, RAGAS Triad, Arena Elo, and FActScore

    The unbiased combinatorial $\text{pass}@k$ estimator, RAG faithfulness vs. retrieval recall, Bradley–Terry pairwise Elo, and atomic factuality.
