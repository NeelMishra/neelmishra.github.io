    vLLM Serving & Low-Latency Inference: PagedAttention, Continuous Batching, and Cutting Latency from 24s to 0.8s

    October 2026 · 5 min read

    Why LLM prefill is compute-bound while autoregressive decode is HBM memory-bandwidth-bound, how PagedAttention eliminates $60\text{--}80\%$ KV-cache fragmentation via OS-style virtual block tables, how iteration-level continuous batching and chunked prefill eliminate head-of-line blocking, and the step-by-step waterfall of how we cut production end-to-end latency at AutoPulse CRM from $24.0\text{s}$ to $0.8\text{s}$ ($96.7\%$ reduction).

    Deploying a fine-tuned LLM with naive HuggingFace `model.generate()` and static request batching hits a severe wall in production: contiguous pre-allocated KV-cache buffers waste over $65\%$ of GPU HBM on internal and external fragmentation, forcing small batch sizes ($B \le 4$), queueing stalls, and $20\text{+ second}$ end-to-end latencies under concurrent dealership traffic.

    $$\text{Bytes}_{\text{KV}}(S, B) = 2 \cdot L \cdot n_{\text{kv}} \cdot d_{\text{head}} \cdot \text{bytes}(\text{dtype}) \cdot S \cdot B.$$
