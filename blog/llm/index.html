    LLM: Large Language Models Reading Guide

    October 2026 · Reading Guide & Series Index

    Training, fine-tuning, and deploying Large Language Models sits at the intersection of numerical representation, optimization dynamics, and GPU memory hierarchy. This section collects focused series on modern LLM methodology—from parameter-efficient fine-tuning over frozen quantized weights (QLoRA) to evaluation benchmarks and low-precision arithmetic.
