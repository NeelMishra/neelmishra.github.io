    FAQ

    Is word error rate a judgment of meaning?

    No. It counts word insertions, deletions, and substitutions against a reference transcript, then divides by the number of reference words. A wrong name and a wrong function word can cost the same unless you inspect the errors.

    What problem does CTC solve?

    A recording has many more frames than words, and the frame alignment is unknown. CTC allows blank steps and repeated labels so the model can be trained without a hand-marked alignment.

    Is HuBERT ordinary next-word supervision?

    No. It predicts discovered audio units, produced by clustering, from masked audio. That is separate from training a decoder to emit words.

    Why does the chapter separate RNN-T from a full-utterance encoder-decoder?

    A streaming model has to produce text before the utterance is finished. An encoder that waits for the whole recording can use future audio that a streaming system does not yet have.
