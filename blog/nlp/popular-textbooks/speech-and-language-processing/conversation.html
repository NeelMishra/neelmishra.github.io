    FAQ

    Is this chapter a complete dialogue-system design?

    No. The draft says it is an initial stub. It introduces conversational structure and points toward annotation. It does not specify a full system.

    What is grounding in this chapter?

    A speaker signals understanding, or shows that understanding failed, during the conversation. It is not the retrieval-grounding task from the information-retrieval chapter.

    What is a dialogue act?

    A label for what an utterance is doing in the conversation, such as a question, answer, or confirmation. It is not the same as a syntactic sentence type.

    Why does initiative matter?

    Participants shift who is driving the task. A system that always waits, or always leads, is implementing a policy. The chapter separates that property from the words alone.
