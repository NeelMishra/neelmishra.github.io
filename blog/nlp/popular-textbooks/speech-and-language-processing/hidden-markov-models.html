    FAQ

    Which algorithm answers which question?

    Forward computes the probability of the observations. Viterbi finds the best state sequence. Forward-backward estimates the parameters from observations. Do not use the likelihood algorithm as a decoder.

    Why is the forward probability not the Viterbi probability?

    Forward sums over paths. Viterbi keeps the single best path. A sequence can be likely in total while its best path is not the one you would read off from the summed score.

    What does forward-backward need?

    Observation sequences and a model structure. It does not need gold state labels. If you already have labeled states, ordinary supervised counts are a different estimator.

    Where does Chapter 18 use this appendix?

    Chapter 18 applies the HMM to part-of-speech tagging. This appendix is the algorithm. The tagging chapter is the application.
