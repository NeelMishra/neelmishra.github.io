    FAQ

    Is this the same chapter as Chapter 19?

    No. Chapter 19 is the parsing chapter. This appendix is the grammar and treebank background, including a sample of English rules.

    Does a treebank replace a handwritten grammar?

    It can be read as a grammar: the rules are the productions that occur in the trees. Coverage and probability still depend on what the treebank annotated.

    What is a head used for?

    It marks the word or child that carries the phrase. Head-finding is a procedure over a tree. It is not itself a parser.

    When are two grammars equivalent?

    When they license the same language, or the same trees, depending on the notion of equivalence in the section. A normal-form grammar can be equivalent without looking like the original rules.
