    FAQ

    Is it leakage for the first target position to attend to the end of the source?

    No, when the full source sentence is the input. The forbidden information is a future target token. Streaming translation has a different source boundary.

    Does a high character-overlap score prove the translation kept a negation?

    No. A short added or missing negation can reverse the meaning and leave most character n-grams intact. Overlap is not adequacy.

    What failure is beam search meant to reduce?

    A locally likely first token can lead to a worse complete translation. A beam keeps several prefixes so a slightly worse start can survive.

    What is a typological divergence?

    A systematic difference the translation has to handle, such as word order, a lexical split, or information one language must state and the other can omit.
