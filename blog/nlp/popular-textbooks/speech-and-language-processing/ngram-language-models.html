    FAQ

    Why is a conditional probability not the unigram count divided by the corpus size?

    Conditioning restricts the denominator to continuations of that history. The corpus-wide count answers a different question.

    How can an unseen sentence still have positive probability?

    The model multiplies local n-gram probabilities. The full sentence may be new even when every counted transition occurred.

    Does adding a constant only to zero cells fix the row?

    Not by itself. The row must still sum to one. A smoothing rule has to define the whole distribution, then renormalize if needed.

    Does lower perplexity mean the system is better?

    Only as a prediction score, and only when both systems use the same test text, tokenization, vocabulary, and boundary convention. It does not measure truth or task success.
