    FAQ

    What does a PCFG add to a context-free grammar?

    A probability for each rule. The grammar can then prefer one licensed tree over another. It does not remove ambiguity by itself.

    Why is lexical insensitivity a problem?

    The same rule gets the same probability regardless of the words. Attachment preferences often depend on the head words. A plain PCFG cannot express that.

    What is nonterminal splitting for?

    It creates finer symbols so contexts that a coarse label treated as the same can have different rule probabilities. The split is a modeling choice, not a new linguistic category.

    How is this appendix related to Chapter 19?

    Chapter 19 introduces constituency, CKY, and span-based neural parsing. This appendix is the probabilistic and lexicalized parser line.
