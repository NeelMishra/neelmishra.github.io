    FAQ

    Is a syntactic object always the same semantic role?

    No. Alternations move the same participant into different syntactic positions. Role labeling has to follow the participant, not the grammatical slot alone.

    How do PropBank and FrameNet differ?

    PropBank roles are defined relative to a verb's frameset. FrameNet roles belong to a semantic frame that can be evoked by more than one verb. A PropBank Arg0 is not automatically a FrameNet role.

    What has to match for a role to be correct?

    Usually the predicate, the argument span, and the role label. A right span with the wrong role, or a right role on the wrong span, is not a full match.

    What is a selectional preference?

    A tendency for a predicate to take arguments of certain kinds, stronger or weaker rather than a hard grammatical rule. It is evidence, not a proof that an unseen combination is impossible.
