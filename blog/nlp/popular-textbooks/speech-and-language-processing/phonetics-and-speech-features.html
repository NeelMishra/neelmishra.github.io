    FAQ

    Does a higher bit depth prevent aliasing?

    No. Bit depth quantizes amplitude. Aliasing comes from sampling too slowly for the frequencies in the signal. Filter or sample faster to control it.

    Does doubling the fundamental frequency double the formants?

    No. Fundamental frequency sets the harmonic spacing of the source. Formants are resonances of the vocal-tract filter. The chapter treats them as separate.

    Why can a one-second recording yield fewer frames than the hop size suggests?

    Each frame needs a full window, not just a hop. Without padding, the last start position is the last one that still has a complete window inside the recording.

    Does zero-padding a frame add observed speech?

    No. It adds zeros so the transform can use a chosen length. It samples the same finite frame on a denser frequency grid.
