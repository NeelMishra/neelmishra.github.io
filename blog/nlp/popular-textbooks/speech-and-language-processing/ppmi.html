    Appendix J

    PPMI

    September 2026 · Study outline

    Appendix: PPMI, in Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin, Speech and Language Processing, 3rd edition draft, August 19, 2026. The posted file is a short note and does not include the usual draft header.

    Topics to learn

    • Pointwise mutual information
      • How often two events co-occur compared with independence
      • PMI between a target word and a context word
    • Positive PMI
      • Why negative PMI is unstable unless the corpus is enormous
      • Replacing negative values with zero
      • PPMI as a weight for word-word matrices, not document counts

    FAQ

    What does a PMI of zero mean?

    The two events co-occur exactly as often as independence would predict. Positive PMI means more than chance. Negative PMI means less.

    Why drop negative PMI?

    A negative value claims the words co-occur less than chance. Estimating that reliably for rare words needs a huge corpus. PPMI sets those values to zero.

    Is PPMI the same as TF-IDF?

    No. TF-IDF weights a term in a document. PPMI weights the association between two words in a word-word matrix. The dimensions are different.

    Does a high PPMI prove a semantic relation?

    No. It is a count association. Frequent named pairs, genres, and errors can score high. The appendix presents it as a weighting function, not a relation labeler.
