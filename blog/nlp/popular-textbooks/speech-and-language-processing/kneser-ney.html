    FAQ

    What is being discounted?

    A fixed amount is subtracted from observed n-gram counts. The saved mass is what smoothing can give to unseen n-grams. The discount is not a new corpus.

    Why not use the raw unigram as the backoff probability?

    A word can be common but only after a few histories. Kneser-Ney estimates the lower-order probability from the number of distinct histories a word continues, not only from how often the word occurs.

    Does interpolation still use the higher-order count?

    Yes. Interpolation mixes the discounted higher-order estimate with the lower-order distribution. It does not throw away an observed n-gram.

    Is a lower perplexity on training data evidence the discount worked?

    No. The discount is judged on held-out text. Training counts are what the discount is correcting.
