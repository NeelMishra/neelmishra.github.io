    FAQ

    Is a discourse relation the same as a sentence connective?

    No. A connective can mark a relation, but many relations have no explicit marker. Parsing has to recover the relation, not only the word.

    What is an elementary discourse unit?

    The span RST parsing treats as a leaf. Segmentation decides those spans before the tree is built. A wrong span makes the later relation comparison ambiguous.

    What does an entity grid measure?

    How entities appear, and in which grammatical roles, across neighboring sentences. It is a local coherence signal. It does not by itself recover a rhetorical tree.

    Does local coherence imply a coherent document?

    No. Neighboring sentences can be locally smooth while the document's global claim structure is broken. The chapter treats global coherence separately.
