    FAQ

    Is a positive lexicon hit the same as a positive sentence?

    No. Composition, negation, and the target of the sentiment can reverse or redirect a word's prior polarity. The lexicon is a feature, not the classifier.

    What does label propagation assume?

    That words in similar contexts, or linked by a similarity graph, often share an affect label. The assumption fails when neighbors are opposites or when the seed labels are narrow.

    Why use a Dirichlet prior in a log-odds comparison?

    Raw counts make rare words look extreme. The prior pulls sparse counts toward a background rate so a word seen twice is not treated like a stable signal.

    What is a connotation frame adding?

    It records who is judged, and how, in a predicate's arguments. A word can be negative for one participant and not for another. A single polarity score hides that.
