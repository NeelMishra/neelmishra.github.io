    Graph Shortest Paths: Algorithm Selector & Reading Guide

    October 2026 · 5 min read · Handwritten Notes

    Overview & decision guide for all 5 handwritten shortest-path study notes: LeetCode 1976, Min-Heap Dijkstra (G-32), A* Search, Bellman–Ford (G-41), and Floyd–Warshall (G-42)

    Every shortest-path algorithm solves the same fundamental question — what is the minimum total weight of a walk from $u$ to $v$? — using the same atomic operation: edge relaxation ($\text{dist}[v] \leftarrow \min(\text{dist}[v],\, \text{dist}[u] + w)$). What separates one algorithm from another is the order in which edges are relaxed, which in turn depends on three properties of your input graph:

    1. Query scope: Single-source to all nodes (SSSP), single-source to a single spatial target, or all-pairs (APSP)?
    2. Edge weights: Unit ($1$), non-negative ($w \ge 0$), or arbitrary signed weights ($w \in \mathbb{R}$, possibly with negative cycles)?
    3. Graph scale: Sparse ($E \approx V$ up to $10^5$) or small dense ($V \le 450$)?

    2. Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix

    Comparison of shortest-path algorithms by scope, weight constraints, negative-cycle handling, and complexity
    Algorithm Scope Edge Weights Detects Negative Cycle? Time Complexity Auxiliary Space
    BFS Single-Source (all targets) Unweighted ($w = 1$) No $\mathcal{O}(V + E)$ $\mathcal{O}(V)$
    Dijkstra (Min-Heap) Single-Source (all targets) Non-negative ($w \ge 0$) No (infinite loop on negative cycles; wrong/exponential with $w \lt 0$) $\mathcal{O}(E \log V)$ $\mathcal{O}(V + E)$
    Dijkstra + ways[] DP Single-Source + Shortest-Path Count Strictly positive ($w \gt 0$) No $\mathcal{O}(E \log V)$ $\mathcal{O}(V + E)$
    A* Heuristic Search Single-Source to Single Goal Non-negative ($w \ge 0$) + admissible $h(n)$ No $\mathcal{O}(E \log V)$ worst-case; prunes search space toward goal $\mathcal{O}(V)$
    Bellman–Ford Single-Source (all targets) Arbitrary ($w \ge 0$ or $w \lt 0$) Yes — via a $V$-th relaxation pass $\mathcal{O}(V \cdot E)$ $\mathcal{O}(V)$
    Floyd–Warshall All-Pairs (every $i \to j$) Arbitrary ($w \ge 0$ or $w \lt 0$) Yes — check diagonal $\text{cost}[i][i] \lt 0$ $\mathcal{O}(V^3)$ $\mathcal{O}(V^2)$
    Crucial Undirected Negative-Edge Trap In an undirected graph, an edge $u \leftrightarrow v$ of weight $w$ is equivalent to two directed edges $u \to v$ and $v \to u$ both of weight $w$. If $w \lt 0$ (say $w = -5$), bouncing $u \to v \to u$ immediately forms a negative cycle of weight $-10$! Therefore, negative-weight shortest paths without negative cycles only occur in directed graphs (unless negative edges are traversed at most once under extra problem constraints).
