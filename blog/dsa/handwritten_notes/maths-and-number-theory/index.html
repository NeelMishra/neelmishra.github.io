    Maths & Number Theory

    October 2026 · 6 min read · 6-Post 1-to-1 Series (11 Handwritten Pages)

    Expanded 1-to-1 from 6 handwritten OneNote notebooks (01_IIIT_MATH_MATERIAL through 06_TUF_SIEVE_OF_ERATOSTHENIS, 11 scanned pages)

    Competitive programming number theory revolves around one recurring trade-off: when can you answer a single query in $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{n})$ or $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ time on the fly, and when do $Q \approx 10^5$ queries force you to invert your loops and precompute a table across $[1, N]$ in $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$ or $\mathcal{O}(N \log \log N)$ time? These six standalone posts expand every one of the six handwritten notebooks in order—preserving every proof, dry run, C++ implementation, and contest edge case.

    1-to-1 Reading Roadmap (All 6 Notebooks)

    01
    IIIT Math Foundations: Division, $\sqrt{n}$ Factoring, CSES 1713 & Euclidean Proof (01_IIIT_MATH_MATERIAL.pdf)

    Euclid's division lemma ($a = bq + r$), unique prime factorization, contradiction proof for $\sqrt{n}$ factor pairs, Codeforces 230B T-Primes ($x = p^2$), CSES 1713 Counting Divisors in $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$ via the harmonic sum $H_N$, prime-exponent $\min/\max$ views of GCD/LCM, and divisor-set invariance of $\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(b, a \bmod b)$.

     02
    Check for Prime Number (02_TUF_CHECK_FOR_PRIME_NUMBER.pdf)

    From $\mathcal{O}(n)$ brute scan to $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{n})$ factor-pair reflection on $n = 36$, the $6k \pm 1$ wheel speedup, and avoiding the $i^2 \lt n$ prime-square bug and 32-bit i * i overflow.

     03
    GCD of Two Numbers & Euclidean Algorithm (03_TUF_GCD_OF_TWO_NUMBERS.pdf)

    Forward scan vs. backward break, Euclidean subtraction traces ($\gcd(7, 2) \to 1$), modulo compression to $\mathcal{O}(\log(\min(n_1, n_2)))$, and the Page 2 proof of why $\gcd(a, 0) = a$.

     04
    LCM of Two Numbers (04_TUF_LCM_of_two_numbers.pdf)

    Prime-multiset proof of $a \times b = \operatorname{lcm}(a, b) \times \gcd(a, b)$, why (a / gcd(a, b)) * b prevents 64-bit overflow, and folding pairwise across $N \ge 3$ arrays.

     05
    All Divisors of a Number (05_TUF_divisor_of_a_number.pdf)

    Collecting complementary pairs $(i, n/i)$ with the i != q perfect-square guard in $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N} + K \log K)$, plus a sort-free two-bucket pure $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N})$ upgrade.

     06
    Sieve of Eratosthenes: $\mathcal{O}(1)$ Primality Precomputation (06_TUF_SIEVE_OF_ERATOSTHENIS.pdf)

    Crossing out composite multiples on $N = 30$, proving why inner loops start at $j = i \cdot i$ and outer loops stop at $i \cdot i \le N$, and bounding the prime harmonic sum $\mathcal{O}(N \log \log N)$.
    Companion Topic — Prime Factorization For dedicated notes on extracting all prime factors with multiplicity by stripping $2$s first and stepping through odd divisors $i = 3, 5, 7, \dots$ up to $\sqrt{n}$, explore the DSA Handwritten Notes Index.
