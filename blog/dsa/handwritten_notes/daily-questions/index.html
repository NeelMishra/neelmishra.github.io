    LeetCode Daily Questions: Handwritten Scratchpad to Optimal Solutions (9 Classics)

    October 2026 · 6 min read · 3-Part Series (13 Handwritten Pages)

    Expanded from handwritten competitive coding study notes (06_Daily_question: 9 September–October 2023 daily problem notebooks, 13 pages)

    These nine handwritten problem sheets capture authentic contest-style problem solving: writing out binary tables to spot $\mathcal{O}(1)$ bit transitions, testing why naive greedy or substring assumptions break on duplicate letters and 132 subsequences, separating interval endpoints for $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ point-in-interval counting, and proving why non-empty DP base cases and state-space bounds save $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ recurrences from edge-case bugs and TLEs.

    Reading Roadmap

    01
    Part 1: Bit DP, Greedy Deduplication & Monotonic Stacks (LC 338, LC 316, LC 456)

    4-bit table recurrences for Counting Bits, from two-stack swaps to last-occurrence monotonic stacks in Remove Duplicate Letters, and the $\mathcal{O}(n^3) \to \mathcal{O}(n^2) \to \mathcal{O}(n)$ journey for 132 Pattern.

     02
    Part 2: Boundary Binary Search & Interval Event Counting (LC 34, LC 2251)

    Dual lower/upper binary search without 2-element midpoint loops in LC 34, and decoupling bloom starts from bloom ends via upper_bound - lower_bound in Number of Flowers in Full Bloom.

     03
    Part 3: AM-GM Math, Non-Empty Subsequence DP, Knapsack & Walk Pruning (LC 343, 1458, 2742, 1269)

    AM-GM proof of the factor-of-3 rule in Integer Break, fixing the all-negative base-case trap in Max Dot Product, reducing Painting the Walls to 0/1 Knapsack, and pruning arrLen to min(arrLen, steps) in LC 1269.

    Master Pattern Table Across All 9 Daily Questions

    Summary of core algorithmic patterns, scratchpad breakthroughs, and optimal complexities across all 9 notes.
    Problem Category Scratchpad Observation / Pitfall Time / Space Chapter
    LC 338: Counting Bits Bit Manipulation + DP 0–15 binary table reveals $\text{dp}[i] = \text{dp}[i \gg 1] + (i \,\&\, 1)$ and power-of-2 MSB blocks. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(1)$ aux Part 1
    LC 316: Remove Duplicate Letters Greedy + Monotonic Stack Only pop larger stack top c when lastIdx[c] > i (another copy appears later) and incoming char isn't already in stack. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(|\Sigma|)$ Part 1
    LC 456: 132 Pattern Monotonic Decreasing Stack Adjacent flags fail (subsequence $\ne$ subarray); scanning right-to-left tracks largest valid nums[k] popped by a peak nums[j]. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$ Part 1
    LC 34: First & Last Position Binary Search On match nums[mid] == target, save mid and keep bisecting left (R = mid - 1) vs. right (L = mid + 1); verified on [2, 2]. $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(1)$ Part 2
    LC 2251: Flowers in Full Bloom Intervals + Binary Search Active intervals at time $t$ = $(\text{starts} \le t) - (\text{ends} \lt t)$ via two sorted endpoint arrays. $\mathcal{O}((n + q)\log n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$ Part 2
    LC 343: Integer Break AM-GM Math / 1D DP AM-GM $\frac{N}{k} \ge \sqrt[k]{\prod a_i}$ peaks near $e \approx 2.718 \implies$ split into $3$s, replacing $3 + 1$ with $2 \times 2$ when $N \equiv 1 \pmod 3$. $\mathcal{O}(1)$ math or $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ DP Part 3
    LC 1458: Max Dot Product 2D Subsequence DP Returning $0$ at base case breaks on [-1, -1] vs [1, 1]! Must return $-\infty$ at boundary and take prod + max(0, dp[i+1][j+1]). $\mathcal{O}(mn)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$ Part 3
    LC 2742: Painting the Walls 0/1 Knapsack DP Greedy cost / time ties fail; paying for wall $i$ buys $1 + \text{time}[i]$ total walls (1 paid + $\text{time}[i]$ free) at price $\text{cost}[i]$. $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$ Part 3
    LC 1269: Ways to Stay in Same Place Bounded Walk DP arrLen reaches $10^6$ while steps $\le 500$: clamp max reachable index to $\min(\text{arrLen} - 1, \lfloor \text{steps}/2 \rfloor)$. $\mathcal{O}(S \cdot \min(L, S))$ / $\mathcal{O}(\min(L, S))$ Part 3
