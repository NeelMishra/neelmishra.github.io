    LeetCode Daily Questions: Handwritten Scratchpad to Optimal Solutions (9 Classics)

    October 2026 · 6 min read · 9-Post 1-to-1 Series (13 Handwritten Pages)

    Expanded 1-to-1 from 9 handwritten competitive coding study notebooks (06_Daily_question: September–October 2023 daily problem sheets, 13 scanned pages)

    Each of these nine standalone posts expands one original handwritten LeetCode daily notebook from scratchpad intuition to optimal C++ implementations: spotting $\mathcal{O}(1)$ bit DP transitions from a 4-bit table, diagnosing why naive greedy or adjacent-element checks break on duplicate letters and 132 subsequences, proving the AM–GM factor-of-3 rule, catching non-empty DP base-case traps, decoupling interval endpoints for online binary search, and pruning $10^6$-wide walk states down to $\lfloor \text{steps} / 2 \rfloor$.

    1-to-1 Reading Roadmap (All 9 Notebooks)

    01
    LC 338: Counting Bits (01_338_Counting_bits.pdf)

    Deriving right-shift + LSB, power-of-2 MSB block offset, and Kernighan $\mathcal{O}(1)$ bit-DP recurrences directly from the $0 \dots 15$ 4-bit binary table.

     02
    LC 316: Remove Duplicate Letters (02_316.pdf)

    Why delayed two-stack deletion yields "adbc" instead of "acdb" on "cbacdcbc", and how an eager monotonic stack with lastIdx solves it in $\mathcal{O}(n)$.

     03
    LC 456: 132 Pattern (03_456.pdf)

    The complete 5-stage progression across 3 handwritten pages: subsequence vs. substring pitfall, $\mathcal{O}(n^3)$ brute force, index sorting, min_from_left $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$, and right-to-left monotonic stack $\mathcal{O}(n)$.

     04
    LC 343: Integer Break (04_343.pdf)

    Log-sum derivatives and AM–GM inequality $\frac{N}{k} \ge \sqrt[k]{\prod a_i}$ proving the $e \approx 2.718 \implies 3$ rule, remainder-mod-3 cases, and $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ partition DP.

     05
    LC 1458: Max Dot Product of Two Subsequences (05_1458.pdf)

    Exposing the empty-subsequence base-case bug on [-1, -1] vs. [1, 1] and fixing it with $-\infty$ boundary sentinels plus max(0, dp[i+1][j+1]).

     06
    LC 34: Find First and Last Position in Sorted Array (06_34.pdf)

    Dual monotonic boundary binary searches across duplicate plateaus without falling into the 2-element [2, 2] floor-midpoint infinite loop.

     07
    LC 2251: Number of Flowers in Full Bloom (07_2251.pdf)

    Decoupling $10^9$-range inclusive intervals into sorted starts and ends arrays to compute bloomed minus debloomed in $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ per query.

     08
    LC 2742: Painting the Walls (08_2742.pdf)

    Why equal cost / time ratios on [1, 2, 3, 2] defeat greedy heuristics, and how $1 + \text{time}[i]$ coverage reduces the problem to 1D 0/1 Knapsack.

     09
    LC 1269: Number of Ways to Stay in the Same Place (09_1269.pdf)

    Tracing the 3-way Left / Right / Stay recursion tree and clamping arrLen ($10^6$) down to $\min(\text{arrLen} - 1, \lfloor \text{steps} / 2 \rfloor) \le 250$.

    Master Pattern & Complexity Table

    Core algorithmic patterns, scratchpad breakthroughs, and optimal complexities across all 9 standalone posts.
    # Problem Pattern Key Scratchpad Insight Optimal Time / Space
    01 LC 338: Counting Bits Bit Manipulation + DP 0–15 binary table reveals $\text{dp}[i] = \text{dp}[i \gg 1] + (i \bmod 2)$. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(1)$ aux
    02 LC 316: Remove Duplicate Letters Greedy + Monotonic Stack Pop larger stack top c iff lastIdx[c] > i; skip incoming char if already inStack. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(|\Sigma|)$
    03 LC 456: 132 Pattern Monotonic Decreasing Stack Scan right-to-left tracking largest third ($\text{nums}[k]$) popped by a taller peak $\text{nums}[j]$. $\mathcal{O}(n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$
    04 LC 343: Integer Break AM–GM Math / 1D DP $(N/k)^k$ peaks at $e \approx 2.718 \implies$ split into $3$s, replacing $3 \times 1$ with $2 \times 2$ when $N \equiv 1 \pmod 3$. $\mathcal{O}(\log N)$ / $\mathcal{O}(1)$
    05 LC 1458: Max Dot Product 2D Subsequence DP Return $-\infty$ out-of-bounds and compute prod + max(0, dp[i+1][j+1]) to reject empty subsequences. $\mathcal{O}(mn)$ / $\mathcal{O}(\min(m,n))$
    06 LC 34: First & Last Position Boundary Binary Search Save ans = mid and keep squeezing R = mid - 1 vs. L = mid + 1; verified on [2, 2]. $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(1)$
    07 LC 2251: Flowers in Full Bloom Intervals + Binary Search Active flowers at $t$ = $(\text{starts} \le t) - (\text{ends} \lt t)$ via upper_bound - lower_bound. $\mathcal{O}((n + q)\log n)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$
    08 LC 2742: Painting the Walls 0/1 Knapsack DP Paying cost[i] buys $1 + \text{time}[i]$ total walls ($1$ paid $+ \text{time}[i]$ free); target sum $\ge n$. $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ / $\mathcal{O}(n)$
    09 LC 1269: Ways to Stay Horizon-Pruned Walk DP Clamp reachable position to $\min(\text{arrLen} - 1, \lfloor \text{steps} / 2 \rfloor)$ so $10^6$ shrinks to $\le 250$. $\mathcal{O}(S \cdot \min(L,S))$ / $\mathcal{O}(\min(L,S))$
