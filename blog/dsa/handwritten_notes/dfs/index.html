    Depth-First Search (DFS) on Trees: BST Rebalancing & Iterative Stack Mechanics

    October 2026 · 5 min read · 2-Note Series (4 Handwritten Pages)

    Expanded 1-to-1 from handwritten competitive coding study notes: 01_1382_Balance_a_binary_search_tree.pdf & 02_DFS_Algorithm_Trees.pdf

    Depth-First Search (DFS) plunges all the way down a branch to a leaf before backtracking to explore sibling subtrees. Because a binary tree is a connected acyclic graph where every child has a unique parent, top-down DFS needs no visited array—making it the natural engine for sorted BST serialization, root-to-leaf path accumulation, and recursive subtree structural checks. Each handwritten PDF in this folder has its own dedicated walkthrough below.
