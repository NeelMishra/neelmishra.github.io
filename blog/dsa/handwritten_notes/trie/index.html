    Trie (Prefix Tree): From String Dictionaries to 32-Bit Maximum XOR

    October 2026 · 6 min read · 3 Parts (16 Handwritten Pages)

    Expanded from 9 handwritten OneNote notebooks (Striver Trie Lectures 1–6 & LeetCode 211, 1023, 421)

    A Trie (prefix tree) replaces whole-key hashing with character-by-character or bit-by-bit tree transitions, turning shared prefixes into shared memory paths. Across competitive programming and interviews, nearly every Trie problem reduces to choosing one of three node layouts—each storing just enough metadata at a node to answer exact-match, prefix-frequency, substring-uniqueness, or bitwise greedy queries in time proportional to word length $L$ (or bit-width $32$).
