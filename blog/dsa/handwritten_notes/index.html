    Competitive Coding & DSA Handwritten Notes

    October 2026 · Master Index · 24 Topic Folders · 116 Notebooks (255 Handwritten Pages)

    Transcribed, structured, and expanded from 24 OneNote competitive coding notebooks, with clean C++/Python templates, step-by-step dry runs, complexity proofs, and expandable scans of every handwritten page.

    This collection organizes 116 handwritten DSA and competitive programming study notebooks into 24 self-contained topic modules. Every chapter preserves the original brute-force $\to$ better $\to$ optimal reasoning, dry-run examples, and edge-case pitfalls from the handwritten notes while pairing them with clean implementations and collapsible page scans.

    1. Arrays, Sorting, Search, Two Pointers & Number Theory

    01
    Arrays & Classic Two-Pointer Water Problems (13 Notebooks)

    Striver Array Lectures 1–6, 8–9 & 11, Trapping Rainwater, Container With Most Water, Wiggle Subsequence, and Pingala's (Pascal's) Triangle.

     02
    Sorting Algorithms (5 Notebooks)

    Bubble Sort, Selection Sort, Insertion Sort, Merge Sort, and Quick Sort with stability, recurrence relations, and partition traces.

     03
    Binary Search — The Monotonic Predicate Template (1 Notebook)

    Unifying boundary binary search and binary search on the answer via a single monotonic condition(mid) invariant.

     04
    Sliding Window & Two Pointers (3 Notebooks · 19 Pages)

    Constant-window templates, variable-length subarray patterns, exact-count reductions, and 16 pages of worked sliding-window problems.

     05
    Monotonic Stack — Generic Template & Duplicate Edge Cases (1 Notebook)

    The 4 monotonic stack variants, amortized $\mathcal{O}(n)$ proof, NGE/PGE/NSE/PSE traces, and the [2, 2] duplicate trap.

     06
    Recursion — Binary Exponentiation Pow(x, n) (1 Notebook)

    Computing $x^n$ in $\mathcal{O}(\log |n|)$ via exponent halving and handling the signed 32-bit INT_MIN overflow edge case.

     07
    Maths & Number Theory (6 Notebooks)

    IIIT Math foundations, $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{n})$ primality & divisor enumeration, Euclidean GCD & LCM, and the $\mathcal{O}(n \log \log n)$ Sieve of Eratosthenes.

     08
    Prime Factorization in $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{n})$ (1 Notebook)

    Extracting 2s, odd prime factors up to $\sqrt{n}$, and the leftover prime factor $> \sqrt{n}$.

    2. Strings, Trees, Tries & Backtracking

    09
    Strings — Knuth–Morris–Pratt (KMP) & LPS Prefix Function (1 Notebook)

    Why $\mathcal{O}(n^3)$ prefix checks collapse to $\mathcal{O}(n)$ via j = lps[j - 1], plus sentinel pattern + '$' + text matching.

     10
    Trees — Morris $\mathcal{O}(1)$-Space Traversal (1 Notebook)

    Temporary predecessor threading for $\mathcal{O}(n)$ time, $\mathcal{O}(1)$ space inorder and preorder binary tree traversal.

     11
    DFS on Trees & Balancing a Binary Search Tree (2 Notebooks)

    Tree DFS recursion templates, subtree question bank, and LeetCode 1382 (inorder extraction + balanced midpoint build).

     12
    Trie & Bitwise Maximum-XOR Trie (9 Notebooks)

    Standard prefix tries, countWordsEqualTo, wildcard search, complete strings, distinct substrings, and 32-bit XOR maximization (LC 421).

     13
    Backtracking & State-Space Search (14 Notebooks)

    Subsets I/II, Permutations, Combination Sum I–IV & Target Sum, Word Search, Phone Letters, Palindrome Partitioning, N-Queens, and Truth-Value Propagation.

    3. Graphs, Connectivity, Spanning Trees & Shortest Paths

    14
    Breadth-First Search (BFS) Level-Order Traversal (1 Notebook)

    Queue invariants, why nodes must be marked visited on enqueue, and $\mathcal{O}(V + E)$ complexity.

     15
    Graph Cycle Detection — Undirected & Directed via DFS & BFS (4 Notebooks)

    Parent-tracking in undirected graphs vs. recursion-path pathVis[] and Kahn's topological count in directed graphs.

     16
    Bipartite Graphs — 2-Coloring with BFS & DFS (2 Notebooks)

    Even vs. odd cycle characterization and BFS/DFS 2-coloring templates (Striver G-17 & G-18).

     17
    Topological Sort — DFS Post-Order Stack & Kahn's BFS (2 Notebooks)

    DAG linear ordering via DFS finish stack and Kahn's in-degree queue algorithm.

     18
    Disjoint Set Union (DSU / Union-Find) & 10 Problem Patterns (11 Notebooks)

    Path compression, union by rank/size, islands/enclaves/redundant edges, string swap equivalence, prime-factor components (LC 952), and Malware Spread II (LC 928).

     19
    Minimum Spanning Trees — Theory, Prim's & Kruskal's (4 Notebooks)

    Spanning tree properties, greedy min-heap Prim's algorithm, and edge-sorted DSU Kruskal's algorithm.

     20
    Graph Shortest Paths — Dijkstra, A*, Bellman-Ford & Floyd-Warshall (5 Notebooks)

    Priority-queue Dijkstra + counting shortest paths (LC 1976), heuristic A* search, negative-edge Bellman-Ford, and all-pairs Floyd-Warshall.

     21
    Strongly Connected Components, Bridges & Articulation Points (4 Notebooks)

    Kosaraju's two-pass SCC algorithm and Tarjan's tin[] / low[] DFS for bridges (G-55) and articulation points (G-56).
