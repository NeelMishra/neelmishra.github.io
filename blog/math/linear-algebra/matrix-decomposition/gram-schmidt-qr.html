    Gram-Schmidt QR: this example is in the process note

    October 2026 · 1 min read

    The 3 by 2, the product \(R=Q^{\mathsf T}A\), and the triangular solve from playlist video 4 are written with the process they use. The shared vectors, the corrected third unit vector, and both triangular steps are in Gram-Schmidt: subtract the projections, then read off R.

    The result does not change. The columns are \(v_1=(1,1,0)\) and \(v_2=(0,1,-1)\). The process lecture's extra vector has a printed unit vector of length \(1/2\). The unit vector is \((1/\sqrt{3})(1,-1,-1)\).
