    Householder matrices: this proof is in the QR note

    October 2026 · 1 min read

    The definition, both proofs, and the reflection picture from playlist video 3 are written with the QR calculation that uses them. The factors, the corrected 2 by 2, and the linked 3 by 3 are in Householder QR: the reflector, then Q and R.

    The result does not change. \(H=I-2uu^{\mathsf T}\) with \(u\) a unit vector, \(H^{\mathsf T}=H\), and \(H^{-1}=H\). The 2 by 2 prints \(u=(-1/5,2/5)\). That vector has length \(1/\sqrt{5}\). The reflector on the next slide uses \(\sqrt{5}\).
