    Permuted LU: this example is in the LU note

    October 2026 · 1 min read

    The row-swap example from playlist video 7 is written with the no-swap solve it assumes. The factors, the product \(PA=LU\), and both triangular solves are in LU: no swap, then one row swap.

    The result does not change. After swapping rows 2 and 3, \(x=(1,2,-3)\). The step list says \(Ly=b\). The boxed equation is \(LUx=Pb\). Using \(b\) gives \((-14,7,2)\), which satisfies \(Ax=Pb\), not \(Ax=b\).
